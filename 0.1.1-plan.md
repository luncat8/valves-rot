# Rotary valve field: combined implementation plan

## Goal

A file-friendly 10 × 10 cutaway mechanism with independent rotating valves and conserved colored grains that settle, are carried by moving walls, and fall through aligned ports. Use classic local scripts, tab indentation, no build or runtime dependencies.

## 0.1 Geometry as the source of truth

- One geometry layer defines housings, circular rotor clips, port sets, cavities, straight segments and tangent offset-center elbow arcs. Rotor passages are the primitives; the raster picture strokes those primitives, so drawing and collision cannot disagree.
- Support I/L/T/X, blind pockets and hollow 0–4-port variants, including adjacent and opposite pairs.
- A housing channel is a corridor from the rotor rim to the cell edge, extended outward as a pour spout on the top row and as a drain on the bottom row. Channels of neighboring cells meet exactly at the shared edge.
- A port meets the housing through a seam aperture: the passage is clipped at the rotor rim, and the throat is the chord where the rotated passage still covers the housing cross-section. The aperture narrows smoothly to zero as the rotor turns, and a shut seam is solid to any grain.
- Clearance is the largest disc that fits at a point, evaluated against the unions actually present: clipped passages, chamber, seam throat, housing channels and the rim wall beside a shut seam.
- Movement is contact-limited: a sweep samples at half the grain radius and bisects the first blocked sample, so a grain can never cross a shut seam or a wall between two frames.
- A grain left in metal by wall motion is recovered through a verified nearest-air query: candidates are clamp projections onto each eroded primitive, ranked by distance and checked against clearance, so recovery is deterministic, never invents a position, and reports the distance it had to move.
- Every layout is derived from an integer seed, one valve per (i, j), so failures reproduce exactly.

## 0.2 Conservative simulation

- Use a fixed timestep accumulator with bounded catch-up and visibility-resume reset. Substep wall motion and particles together; derive wall velocity from actual elapsed simulation time.
- Solve gravity, low restitution, friction and particle contacts relative to moving walls. Use the geometry sweep for every move so closed ports stay closed.
- Preallocate contact scratch space and spatial bins. Reconcile pair and wall constraints iteratively; handle coincident grains deterministically.
- Seed nonoverlapping colored grains in selected upper reservoirs. Add explicit pour, drain and reset actions. Track injected, active and drained counts; never silently delete or teleport trapped grains.
- Keep analytical clearance queries; evaluate a local cached distance field only if profiling warrants it. Avoid full-frame canvas readback.

## 0.3 Presentation and interaction

- Cache static gray tinted housings and cutaway rotor sprites. Rebuild only for layout, air color or pixel-density changes.
- Share exact black/white air across all openings. Preserve clear material thickness and rounded bends at small viewport sizes.
- Offer pause, single-step, rotation speed, reset, seeded layout and explicit pour controls. Add pointer/keyboard valve selection, visible focus and selection, labeled controls and throttled stats.
- Keep simulation settings separate from DOM state. Use guarded Node exports for geometry and simulation tests.
- Provide a coarse air-model overlay that draws the clearance field a grain resolves, for visual comparison against the picture.

## 0.4 Acceptance and measurements

- Deterministic geometry fixtures: seeded layouts, tangent elbow joins, seam membership under rotation, quarter-turn invariance, continuous rim threads, tight throats, sealed crossings, housing continuity and pour spouts, clearance reference values, no invented air, drawing path equals passage prims.
- Deterministic simulation fixtures: falling straight column, sealed reservoir, quarter-turn elbow, two nearly aligned ports, multi-outlet chamber, jammed cluster, coincident grains and rotating closed pocket.
- Assert finite state, conservation, bounded penetration, no crossing closed seams and equivalent fixed-step results across display frame rates.
- Browser checks: direct file opening, offline loading, narrow viewport, high DPI, both air colors, pause/resume, speed zero, background/resume and keyboard control.
- Record update/render timings at 120, 500 and 1,000 grains, allocation profiles and long-run contact failures in `experiments/logs/`. Target 60 Hz at the default population on the recorded reference environment; report hardware rather than claiming universal performance.
- Compare fixtures visually against all prototype pages before choosing final colors and particle parameters.

## Delivery boundary

Keep geometry, simulation, renderer and controls independent. Retain the prototype gallery as comparison material. Archive this plan only when its acceptance gates have been satisfied; logs belong in a separate worklog.
