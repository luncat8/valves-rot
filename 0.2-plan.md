# Game mode, rotor control and goal quests (0.2)

## Goal

Extend the rotary valve field from a pure sandbox (**Lab mode**) with **Game mode**: competitive and solo routing quests over the shared conservative grain and pressure simulation, supported by adjustable automatic valve rotation, press-drag rotor turning, and player-colored valve housings.

## Rotor control and housing tints

- **Auto-rotate share (`0–100%`)**: a deterministic per-seed permutation of the 100 cells determines which rotors turn automatically on their dwell schedule. Lab mode defaults to `90%`; Game mode defaults to `7%` so player-built routes persist while background motion remains lively. Active quest goal valves never auto-rotate.
- **Press-drag rotation**: pressing on a valve and dragging around its center (or pulling horizontally when starting near the hub) drives a quarter-turn dial. The hand owes one quarter turn (`π/2`) of travel per detent, with `0.22 rad` of grip slack and a half-detent snap before the target steps; the target stays on whole detents while the overlay arc shows the exact pull, so a small wobble rubber-bands back instead of turning the rotor. The hub pull costs `52 px` per detent after `9 px` of slack. A drag starts only after the pointer has moved `6 px`, so a press that is meant as a click still turns once on release; pointer capture tracks the drag outside the cell, suppresses the trailing click turn, and the cursor reads `grab`/`grabbing`/`not-allowed`/`pointer` from what the pointer is over.
- **Player-tinted housings**: each cell carries an optional player color index (`-1` neutral grey, `0..P-1` player tint). Tinted housings render an anodized colored case and bore collar across all skins (`glm`, `combined`, `gpt`) and both air colors without altering collision geometry. Updating one valve or housing tint repaints only the affected sprite or background canvas in place.

## Modes and players

- **Lab mode** (default): sandbox controls enabled (`Place valve` brush, `Ctrl`-click fill, `Drain`, `Pour`, a mouth in every column, `90%` auto-rotate).
- **Game mode**: displays the quest board and competitors list beside the field, switches auto-rotate to `7%`, and disables sandbox-only shortcuts (`Place valve`, `Ctrl`-click fill, global `Drain`) so every participant plays through valve routing, `Pour`, and their own air mouths.
- **Owned mouths**: instead of two ten-mouth bands, Game mode deals each competitor **two in** and **two out** mouths at independent random columns and leaves every other column of either band empty. The in columns come from one shuffled deck dealt to the players in turn, so no two players share an in column; the out columns are dealt by matching each out slot to a distinct column that is not one of that player's own in columns. A player's four mouths therefore always sit in four different columns, and their in and out mouths never share an `x`; the two bands are still independent, so one column can carry a source for one player and a vent for another. That is `2 in` and `2 out` per player, `4 mouths × players` (`8` with four players). An owned mouth answers only to its owner and keeps the polarity it was dealt; a sealed or open state stays the owner's choice, and pressing a rival's mouth or an empty slot reports it in the game status line instead of acting. Rendered as a player-cased disc with an accent ring, a translucent stem down its column and owner-coloured plates on both band edges (redrawn above the grain piles), plus the owner's name and `in`/`out` role on hover. Leaving Game mode restores the full sandbox bands; returning deals a fresh set.
- **Competitors and bot control**: player `0` (`You`) competes alongside bot players, each with a distinct housing color, active quest indicator, completed count, and score. A **Bot control** checkbox enables or pauses bot signing and routing at any time.

## Quests and property-based generator

- Maintain a live list of goals. At most one player may sign a given goal (`owner`), and each player holds at most one signed goal at a time.
- Signing a goal spawns `1` or `2` upright bottle-like goal valves (`hollow single · direction ↑ N · 270°`, port `N`) on routable mid-to-lower cells, tinted with the signing player's housing color and locked against automatic rotation and type replacement. The goal owner may still turn their own bottle manually (for instance, to spill excess grains on an exact-count task).
- A signed grain quest raises a **pour priority** on the mouth column that its route search reaches: `Pour` seeds that column for the whole life of the quest, on top of the columns the seeded layout already opens. The promotion is refcounted per column, so two quests reaching the same column share it and one quest ending cannot strand the other; it is released when the quest is completed, dropped or abandoned, and never survives a reset.
- Generate quests procedurally from orthogonal difficulty properties whose weights determine the tier (**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**) and score reward:
	- **Count precision** (grain quests): wide range (e.g. `2–8`, easy), open minimum (`3+..6+` easy, `9+..12+` medium), narrow range (`5–8`, medium), or exact count (`1` or `7`, hard).
	- **Color hue bands** (grain quests): any color (`0` bands), broad hue sectors (`2` bands of `180°` or `3` bands of `120°`, medium), or single-color `6`-band sectors (`60°`, hard). A bottle satisfies a hue-band requirement either when enough individual grains inside it belong to the band, or when the total grain count meets the target and the circular mean hue of the bottle's grains falls inside the band with sufficient color coherence (`R >= 0.42`).
	- **Air potential** (pressure quests): hold chamber normalized grid potential `> x` or `< x` in central rows where baseline potential starts uncommitted, requiring open passage routing and/or mouth toggling. A cross-shaped bottle opens onto every passage, so a pressure target is only worth what the tier demands of the reachable headroom: the threshold lands `60%` of the way from the current potential to that extreme on easy and medium, `78%` on hard. Signing a pressure quest while pressure is `Off` enables `Grid gas`, and a pressure quest always has **one** chamber: two bottles would sit in two pockets under one shared target. The bottle goes in a row of a column whose opened shaft joins a **pocket** (the air around that column and the band cells at its ends) that the signer's own mouths can press. Columns are ranked by the share of that pocket's boundary the signer owns — sealing an own vent charges a pocket that rivals' vents no longer feed, sealing an own source drains one that rivals' sources no longer fill — and the shaft is opened before anything is measured. The target is then fitted with three converged solves: the field as it stands, the signer's favourable pair open with their opposing pair sealed, and the reverse; every rival mouth stays exactly as it is open or sealed, because that is the field the signer plays around. A tier share of the measured `span` is demanded, so the number printed before signing is only a placeholder for that refit. A placement whose measured span is under `0.10` is retried on another row or column; if none clears it, the widest weak span on the board is played at half aim, and only a board with no lever at all falls back to asking for the side the field already holds. Flipping the generated polarity is the last resort, and one press is still the whole mouth policy: open the pair of the felt polarity and seal the other pair.
	- **Bottle count**: `1` bottle (standard) or `2` bottles that must both be fed (medium/hard, grain quests only).
	- **Time limit**: untimed or a countdown scaled to the quest's own demand — a base clock plus a share per required grain and per extra bottle, with a fixed untimed share of rolls. A tight clock earns the higher difficulty rating rather than more points; expired quests fail cleanly.
- Require the target condition to hold continuously across a brief settle window (`0.45 s` for open `N+` counts, `1.0 s` for bounded/exact counts, `1.6 s` for pressure targets) so falling streams passing transiently through an exact count do not falsely complete it. One window covers a whole quest: with two bottles, every bottle has to hold and any one of them going unsatisfied costs the streak.
- **Quest end**: on completion (awarding points) or expiration/abandonment, replace every goal `hollow single` cell with `solid blank`, clear its housing tint and auto-rotation lock, remove the finished goal from the list, and append a newly generated quest while preserving exact grain conservation (`injected = active + drained + purged`).

## Bot controller

- Bots act on human-paced cooldowns with preallocated routing buffers and zero per-frame allocations, and only on quests that have been open long enough for a human to have seen them: sign an open quest, run a cell-graph search from grain sources or boundary mouths to their goal bottle, turn one misaligned valve along the route (or shut the feeder above a satisfied bounded bottle), trigger `Pour` when upper grains run scarce, and on pressure quests set their own four mouths (favourable pair open, opposing pair sealed) before aligning the vertical shaft into their bottle, without touching another player's mouth or goal cell.

## Validation

- `experiments/game-balance.cjs` runs full matches and reports what the generator actually produces and what a bot earns: per-kind and per-tier completion, expiry, grains poured per completed quest, solve and wait times, the time-limit distribution, and how much of a bottle's volume a hue band needs.
- Deterministic Node fixtures (`experiments/game-fixtures.cjs`): exact auto-rotate percentages and goal locks, housing tint updates across skins, owned-mouth dealing (two in + two out per player, independent in and out columns, one mouth per column per band, no `x` shared by a player's own in and out mouths, owner-only presses, no polarity flip, Lab restore), procedural quest property/tier coverage, `2`/`3`/`6`-band count and circular average-hue matching, single-sign exclusivity, `hollow single · ↑ N` spawning, pocket-ranked pressure bottles, the measured-span retry, and the owned-mouth completion recipe, hold-window completion, `solid blank` replacement, timeout handling, grain conservation, and autonomous bot completion vs disabled bot control.
- Smoke verification (`experiments/render-smoke.cjs`): auto-rotate slider, the drag dial (a `25°` wobble is inert, half a hand turn is two detents, a `30 px` hub pull is inert and `40 px` is one detent) without duplicate click turns, mode switching, quest signing, and zero-allocation frame loop compatibility.
