# Game mode, rotor control and goal quests (0.2)

## Goal

Extend the rotary valve field from a pure sandbox (**Lab mode**) with **Game mode**: competitive and solo routing quests over the shared conservative grain and pressure simulation, supported by adjustable automatic valve rotation, press-drag rotor turning, and player-colored valve housings.

## Rotor control and housing tints

- **Auto-rotate share (`0–100%`)**: a deterministic per-seed permutation of the 100 cells determines which rotors turn automatically on their dwell schedule. Lab mode defaults to `90%`; Game mode defaults to `20%` so player-built routes persist while background motion remains lively. Active quest goal valves never auto-rotate.
- **Press-drag rotation**: pressing on a valve and dragging around its center (or pulling horizontally across detents when starting near the hub) steps its quarter-turn target continuously clockwise or counter-clockwise. Pointer capture tracks the drag outside the cell and suppresses the trailing click turn on release.
- **Player-tinted housings**: each cell carries an optional player color index (`-1` neutral grey, `0..P-1` player tint). Tinted housings render an anodized colored case and bore collar across all skins (`glm`, `combined`, `gpt`) and both air colors without altering collision geometry. Updating one valve or housing tint repaints only the affected sprite or background canvas in place.

## Modes and players

- **Lab mode** (default): sandbox controls enabled (`Place valve` brush, `Ctrl`-click fill, `Drain`, `Pour`, air inlets, `90%` auto-rotate).
- **Game mode**: displays the quest board and competitors list beside the field, switches auto-rotate to `20%`, and disables sandbox-only shortcuts (`Place valve`, `Ctrl`-click fill, global `Drain`) so every participant plays through valve routing, `Pour`, and air-inlet toggling.
- **Competitors and bot control**: player `0` (`You`) competes alongside bot players, each with a distinct housing color, active quest indicator, completed count, and score. A **Bot control** checkbox enables or pauses bot signing and routing at any time.

## Quests and property-based generator

- Maintain a live list of goals. At most one player may sign a given goal (`owner`), and each player holds at most one signed goal at a time.
- Signing a goal spawns `1` or `2` upright bottle-like goal valves (`hollow single · direction ↑ N · 270°`, port `N`) on routable mid-to-lower cells, tinted with the signing player's housing color and locked against automatic rotation and type replacement. The goal owner may still turn their own bottle manually (for instance, to spill excess grains on an exact-count task).
- Generate quests procedurally from orthogonal difficulty properties whose weights determine the tier (**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**) and score reward:
	- **Count precision** (grain quests): wide range (e.g. `2–8`, easy), open minimum (`3+..6+` easy, `9+..12+` medium), narrow range (`5–8`, medium), or exact count (`1` or `7`, hard).
	- **Color hue bands** (grain quests): any color (`0` bands), broad hue sectors (`2` bands of `180°` or `3` bands of `120°`, medium), or single-color `6`-band sectors (`60°`, hard). A bottle satisfies a hue-band requirement either when enough individual grains inside it belong to the band, or when the total grain count meets the target and the circular mean hue of the bottle's grains falls inside the band with sufficient color coherence (`R >= 0.42`).
	- **Air potential** (pressure quests): hold chamber normalized grid potential `> x` or `< x` in central rows where baseline potential starts uncommitted, requiring open passage routing and/or inlet toggling. Signing a pressure quest while pressure is `Off` enables `Grid gas`.
	- **Bottle count**: `1` bottle (standard) or `2` simultaneous bottles (medium/hard).
	- **Time limit**: untimed or bounded countdown (`35–85 s`); expired quests fail cleanly.
- Require the target condition to hold continuously across a brief settle window (`0.45 s` for open `N+` counts, `1.0 s` for bounded/exact counts and pressure targets) so falling streams passing transiently through an exact count do not falsely complete it.
- **Quest end**: on completion (awarding points) or expiration/abandonment, replace every goal `hollow single` cell with `solid blank`, clear its housing tint and auto-rotation lock, remove the finished goal from the list, and append a newly generated quest while preserving exact grain conservation (`injected = active + drained + purged`).

## Bot controller

- Bots act on human-paced cooldowns with preallocated routing buffers and zero per-frame allocations: sign an open quest, run a cell-graph search from grain sources or boundary mouths to their goal bottle, turn one misaligned valve along the route (or shut the feeder above a satisfied bounded bottle), trigger `Pour` when upper grains run scarce, and toggle/flip column mouths on pressure quests without touching another player's goal cell.

## Validation

- Deterministic Node fixtures (`experiments/game-fixtures.cjs`): exact auto-rotate percentages and goal locks, housing tint updates across skins, procedural quest property/tier coverage, `2`/`3`/`6`-band count and circular average-hue matching, single-sign exclusivity, `hollow single · ↑ N` spawning, hold-window completion, `solid blank` replacement, timeout handling, grain conservation, and autonomous bot completion vs disabled bot control.
- Smoke verification (`experiments/render-smoke.cjs`): auto-rotate slider, orbital and hub press-drag gestures without duplicate click turns, mode switching, quest signing, and zero-allocation frame loop compatibility.
