# 0.3 water sources — input source vents

Water gets real input sources: one vent per column, drawn on the canvas in a
strip above the top air mouths, each with its own on/off flag, its own rate and
its own pour colour. The single global inlet becomes the editor of one
"current" vent, and the global Column control becomes a brush that stamps the
current settings onto every vent it passes.

## State and supply

- `ventOn[N]`, `ventRate[N]`, `ventShares[N*3]` are the only new conserved-side
  state: per column an on/off flag, a rate in px²/s and a normalized share
  triple. Defaults: shut, `DEFAULT_RATE`, equal white shares.
- The scalar mirror (`ventOpen`, `rate`, `pour`, `pourMask`) always describes
  `ventColumn`, refreshed by one `syncCurrent` after every mutation, so the
  panel and the old fixture API read one place. `setVent(column, open)`
  selects and sets the flag; `setSupply`/`setPour` edit the current vent;
  `selectVent` selects without touching anything.
- `supply` loops the vents: each open vent deposits `rate*h` of its own shares
  at its own mouth cell through the same `deposit`/books primitives, so the
  per-channel ledger closes for any number of simultaneous sources. A blocked
  mouth is unadmitted per vent; a full system still overflows the top.
- `reset` shuts every vent and keeps each one's rate and colour.
- `RATE_MAX` (the page Rate slider's max) caps every vent rate and is published
  on the water state (`rateMax`) for the renderer and the gestures.

## Gestures (canvas strip, water on)

- The strip is `VENT_STRIP` (16 px) of canvas above the pour band; the mouth
  hit test stops at the band so the strip belongs to the vents alone.
- Click selects the vent as current (no parameter changes).
- A slide arms after the same travel as the valve dial and locks its axis to
  the first dominant movement: left/right sweeps hue, one tab width per full
  revolution; up/down scales the rate, the full range over 60 px of travel.
  The gesture also makes its vent current, so the panel follows the hand.
- Double-click opens a native colour picker prefilled with the vent's
  normalized colour; a pick maps to proportional channel shares. Pure black is
  refused, like an empty pour mask.
- Long-click (hold ~½ s without moving) toggles the vent; the trailing click
  is suppressed, like a finished slide.

## Hue mapping

- `hueShares` maps a hue to the share triple over the six sector corners
  (R, Y, G, C, B, M) with a linear ramp between neighbours; `sharesHue` is the
  inverse for the cursor and the picker's opening value. Grey triples have no
  hue and report 0.
- The R/G/B boxes keep their equal-share semantics on the current vent; a
  hue-picked vent mirrors back as every channel it uses.

## Column brush

- Moving the global Column slider stamps the settings of the vent it left onto
  every vent it passes (flag, rate, shares), ending as the current vent. A jump
  stamps the whole span it crossed. Clicking a tab selects without stamping.

## Rendering

- The background sheet, the frame clear and the canvas all grow by the strip;
  the air and water overlays keep their band-and-field rects.
- One tab per column: a chip in the liquid's normalized colour, a rate gauge
  against `rateMax`, a sealed cross and dimming when shut, the current vent
  outlined, and a hover/drag label. During a hue slide the tab becomes the
  spectrum with a cursor at the live hue. The colour strings are cached per
  vent and rebuilt only when the shares version moves, so the frame loop
  allocates no strings.

## Acceptance

- `experiments/water-sources-fixtures.cjs`: two vents pour different liquids
  into their own columns with the rate ratio kept and the channel ledger
  closed; the single-vent API still pours exactly one column; a shut spout
  confines its own vent while its neighbour pours; the hue wheel corners,
  ramps and wraps both ways, and normalization keeps the hue; direct per-vent
  edits land while the mirror follows the current vent and refusals change
  nothing; the sweep stamps every vent it passes and skips the rest; reset
  shuts all and keeps parameters.
- Existing water fixtures stay green after moving two `setPour` calls after
  their `setVent` (shares are per vent now, so the vent is selected first).
- `render-smoke.cjs` covers the strip geometry, the click/slide/picker wiring
  and the stamped sweep read back through the panel.
