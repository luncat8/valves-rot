# 0.4 grain melting and thermodynamics plan

## Goal and model

Add cell-attached heating/cooling, a temperature view, and reversible grain/liquid phase change with an explicit energy ledger. This is an illustrative local-equilibrium model, not a calibrated thermal fluid simulation. Begin in Lab mode; quests and automated heat control are out of scope. Melting depends on slice 1 of `0.3-water-color-plan.md`; heat diffusion can be developed independently.

Add a classic `heat.js` with guarded Node exports and preallocated typed arrays on the water-grid geometry. Store total enthalpy per grid cell, including sensible energy in fixed metal, liquid and grains, and latent energy of liquid. Derive temperature from enthalpy, composition and material parameters, rather than independently editing temperature and energy. A single temperature per cell assumes rapid local equilibration. Metal exists in solid portions too; use sampled open/solid fractions for capacities and face conductivities, and conduct across metal and fluid faces without crossing a missing domain boundary. Cell ownership maps each heat source to its valve; distribute a valve's signed power over its owned cells by fixed normalized weights so source total is independent of grid resolution. Rotating valves retain source settings; fixed spatial metal heat does not rotate with them in this first model.

Use the existing illustrative scale: 1 mm/px, 10 mm slice depth and 1 mg per simulation mass unit. Define specific heats, latent heats, conductivity, ambient exchange and melting points in a single script-settable parameter table, with explicit units and positive finite defaults; use melting points above the default 20 °C ambient. Effective liquid densities in the color plan govern buoyancy only; for thermal mass use the reference 1000 kg/m³, so a px² liquid slice represents one simulation mass unit and checkbox changes do not create mass or energy. Grain mass is already `rho_grain/1000 * r²` in those units. Document this approximation in UI and Help.

Conduct heat between neighbors with equal and opposite energy transfers, and exchange heat with ambient via signed Newton loss at configured exterior/band faces. Explicit diffusion uses a stability bound computed from conductance and heat capacity; split the fixed simulation step into stable substeps. Set a documented substep budget and reject/clamp parameter changes that would exceed it rather than silently running unstable. No allocation in hot loops. Rebuild geometry/capacity on changes without discarding enthalpy; remap newly closed/open cells deterministically. Keep a ledger of initial energy, signed source input, ambient exchange, imported/exported material enthalpy and current stored energy. Supply enters at ambient with its sensible and latent enthalpy; overflow and drain carry donor enthalpy. Grain crossings move their sensible energy between cells, once per crossing, including during compaction and removal. A disabled or reset state must have explicit ledger boundary semantics. Internal conduction and phase conversion cancel in the ledger.

## Phase change

Map grain materials to liquid channels by mass: red (1,0,0), amber (½,½,0), teal (0,½,½), blue (0,0,1), pink (½,0,½), green (0,1,0), using the actual material indices rather than assuming palette order. A grain of mass `m` melts into `m` px² of reference-density liquid, apportioned by that table; retain channel shares even for separated phases. Remove the grain via the normal compaction path and add the liquid to an eligible open cell or defer if no capacity exists. Transfer its sensible enthalpy, charge `L*m` latent energy, and solve the resulting equilibrium temperature without inventing heat. At the melting point, pin temperature while energy is insufficient to melt a whole grain; retain excess enthalpy as pending latent energy. Deterministic processing and preallocated state avoid frame-order or allocation surprises. A whole-grain threshold is an approximation, not instantaneous melting on touching a heater.

Freeze individual primary channels into their corresponding primary grains when energy is removed below their freezing plateau; process higher melting-point species first with a stable tie break. Release exactly `L*m` into the cell and solve the shared enthalpy balance. Convert a chosen mass to radius `sqrt(m*1000/rho_grain)`; clamp to supported grain radii and accumulate fractional inventory until a valid grain can be placed. Check `slotFree`, geometry clearance, grain capacity and available channel mass before committing; defer without consuming mass or energy when blocked. This preserves total mass and each channel's mass, but secondary grains do not re-form as alloys: amber, teal and pink freeze as primary grains. Round-trip acceptance means conserved composition, mass and energy, not identical particle count or original material. Multiple transition plateaus and latent buffers must be resolved deterministically and included in enthalpy, including when liquid moves or drains.

Run phase change after contacts and compaction, at a documented point relative to water transport; account for grain entry/exit and all material movement exactly once. Add arrays to the shared grain field copy list if per-grain state is required. Explicitly handle drain, water disable, reset, geometry closure, blocked placement and external grain additions/removals in both mass and energy ledgers.

## Controls and display

Heat enable automatically enables Water with its vent closed; turning Water off disables Heat, while per-valve source settings persist. Provide signed power for the selected valve, ambient temperature, temperature-view toggle and energy readouts in a Heat fieldset, with clear units and reset behavior. Render temperature with a fixed, labeled °C color scale and source badges; reuse the existing field-map/legend and geometry mask patterns, not per-texel geometry queries. The overlay is visual only and does not suppress the liquid color settings. Describe local equilibrium, reference units, phase thresholds and ledger boundaries in Help.

## Slices and acceptance gates

1. **Heat field.** Implement conduction, source, ambient loss, moving-grain sensible energy, field view and ledger without phase change. `node experiments/heat-fixtures.cjs`: insulated uniform field remains constant; two-cell exchange is equal/opposite; prescribed source changes energy by power × time; ambient exchange has the correct sign; moving a grain preserves total energy; geometry rebuild, reset and material boundaries balance; stability-limit rejection and finite values are exercised. Compare against analytic two-cell cooling and timestep refinement.
2. **Melting** (requires water-color slice 1). `node experiments/melt-fixtures.cjs`: each material's channel mapping and reference mass; no melt below threshold; energy-limited melting and plateau; blocked capacity defers; grain compaction, transfer and external supply/drain preserve the mass/channel and energy ledgers. Check equality of sensible + latent energy before and after conversion in an insulated cell.
3. **Freezing.** Test latent release, highest-transition-first ordering, minimum/maximum radius and fractional remainder, slot/clearance failure and retry, and hot/cold repeated cycles. Assert primary channel mass and total energy residuals within reported absolute/relative tolerances, including mixed-secondary-to-primary round trips and moving/draining latent inventory.
4. **UI and measurement.** Add `experiments/heat-picture.cjs` for heater, cooling and temperature-legend examples; record fixed-seed scenarios, parameter values, ledgers, timings and tolerances under `experiments/logs/`. Run existing water, simulation, render and game smoke fixtures. Verify controls, water/heat lifecycle, source persistence, badges and `file://` behavior manually.

## Balance and limits

Publish source/ambient/material-boundary terms and maximum residual of `E_final - E_initial - E_source - E_ambient - E_material` for closed and open scenarios; latent energy is internal, never an external source. Report grain + liquid reference mass and each channel's initial, added, removed and final inventories separately. Measure substeps and wet/heat step cost against the existing water baseline, with reproducible seed, duration and machine. Defaults and any stability cap are tuning choices until measured.

No convection, thermal radiation, evaporation, alloy synthesis, heat carried by rotating metal, mechanical work/viscous heating, or independent grain temperatures are modeled. Local equilibrium, whole-grain phase thresholds, grid geometry and fixed reference liquid density limit realism. The existing pressure display is not a thermodynamic pressure measurement; do not claim physical calibration.
