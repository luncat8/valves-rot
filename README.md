# Rotary valve field

Open `index.html` directly in a browser to choose a prototype. No install, build, network access or server is required.

- `prototype/draft.md`: original specification.
- `prototype/comparison.md`: requirements comparison and solver limitations.
- `archive/0.1.1-plan.md`: archived initial combined-version design and acceptance gates.
- `0.2-plan.md`: `v0.2` specification — auto-rotate percentage, press-drag turning, player-tinted housings, Game mode, composable quest generator and bot competitors.
- `pressure-plan.md`: inlet boundaries, air backends, ramp calibration and validation limits.
- `water-plan.md`: phased plan for a water inlet, rising level and buoyancy, with falling droplets deferred.
- `prototype/shared/`: combined implementation. `geometry.js` is the single source of truth for housings, passages, seam apertures and clearance; `simulation.js` advances conserved grains through `sweep` with moving-wall contact and spatial bins; `game.js` manages Lab/Game modes, quests, hue-band matching and bots; `render.js` strokes those same primitives, paints player-tinted housings and batches grains.
- `experiments/`: Node validation and timing scripts (`geometry-check.cjs`, `geometry-fixtures.cjs`, `simulation-fixtures.cjs`, `gas-fixtures.cjs`, `air-fixtures.cjs`, `calibration-fixtures.cjs`, `game-fixtures.cjs`, `render-smoke.cjs`, `simulation-bench.cjs`, `behavior-probe.cjs`, `front-probe.cjs`, `aperture-probe.cjs` and `aperture-picture.cjs`).

Click any valve, press-drag around its rim (or horizontally across its hub), or focus the canvas and use arrow keys plus Enter/Space (Shift to reverse), to turn a selected valve by 90°. **Auto-rotate** sets the deterministic percentage of valves that schedule automatic quarter-turns (`90%` default in Lab mode, `20%` in Game mode). Rotation speed controls valves, not gravity. Static help lives in the **Help** drawer (`?`), which lists every shortcut. The **Place valve** drawer (`V`) is a brush for double-click (or `P` on the selected valve): pick a valve type, direction, and/or player case tint — each left unselected to keep the current one (click a selected button again to unselect) — copy any field valve's type and direction into the brush, or copy a type name (with the selected direction, when one is selected) to the clipboard as text for chat. Changing only the direction or case tint keeps grains; changing the type clears that cell.

Switch **Mode** (`G`) between **Lab** (free sandbox) and **Game**: a 4-player scoreboard (**You** plus three bots toggled by **Bot control**) and a live 5-goal board. Only one competitor can sign onto each goal. Signing spawns `1–2` player-tinted goal bottles on the field (grain quests use `'hollow single · direction ↑ N · 270° (ports N)'`, pressure quests use a four-way `'hollow cross'` with an open air shaft in its column); route grains from spouts (`Pour`) or steer air vents until the bottle satisfies its target for the hold window, then the bottle seals into a `'solid blank'` plug, awards points, and spawns a new procedural quest. Quests combine difficulty properties: wide vs narrow count ranges, large counts, exact counts (`1` or `7`), timers, `2`/`3`/`6`-band color groups (allowing circular-mean average hue inside the target band), and high/low air-pressure thresholds (`> x` or `< x`).

Air is controlled by the mouths in two bands **outside** the 10×10 field: every column has one inlet above (low) and one below (high). Click a mouth to seal or open it, Shift-click to swap its polarity. **Grid gas** (default) solves a conservative diffusion of relative pressure on a pipe network and shows it as a blue-to-red overlay; an open mouth holds its cells at exactly `1` or `0`, and air only crosses links that a small clearance disc can actually travel through. Every valve's passages are divided into cells in **the rotor's own unrotated coordinates**, while the short pipes in its casing stay fixed; a turn recomputes only the seam links between them, so a part-turned rotor throttles its ports continuously and carries its air with the passage instead of having the mouths repaint it. A packed pile raises the resistance of its cells and slows the modeled flow. **Air particles** shows a cyan kinetic population that circulates from bottom sources to top vents and knocks grains about. **Air force** scales the push from 0 (grains behave as with air off) to 2 (updrafts can hold a grain). **Off** leaves the grains untouched. Switching modes does not reset grains or valve targets.

Closing **every** vent no longer floods the field to one flat colour. A mouth click only changes the boundary; the next 1/30 s ticks carry a pressure front through the open pipes, so near cells fill before far ones — a connected machine fills in about a third of a second, whether its rotors are on a detent or caught mid-turn. A pocket cut off by shut valves keeps the air it already has, and if all mouths are sealed the whole machine holds its pattern with no pressure through-flow — though a turning rotor still stirs the air inside its own disc and drags it one cell out through each open port mouth, and the overlay and its scale stay visible. Turning a rotor changes the seam links instead of repainting: the air inside the rotor turns with it.

The grid legend displays **equivalent gauge Pa**, not a measured local pressure: it maps the normalized 0–1 pressure to an editable reference span. The default 0.7125 Pa span comes from a 20-second particle-wall impulse measurement in an idealized reference chamber, using 1 mm/px, 1 s per simulation second, 1 mg per mass unit and 10 mm depth. It does not change the simulation forces, establish atmospheric pressure, or make the grid a thermodynamic model. See `pressure-plan.md` for the pipe network, transient solver, analytical seam checks and limitations. Run `node experiments/calibration-fixtures.cjs`, `node experiments/gas-fixtures.cjs`, `node experiments/air-fixtures.cjs`, and `node experiments/game-fixtures.cjs` for the checks; `experiments/logs/grid-gas-valve-cells.txt` contains the current network, front and cost measurements; `simulation-pressure.txt` holds earlier ones.
