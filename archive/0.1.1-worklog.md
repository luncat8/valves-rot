# Shared geometry and seam contact

## Review

The two converted prototypes each carried a private geometry: GLM Max an analytical `isAir` with separate per-primitive tests, GPT a raster mask read back per frame. Their passage widths, clip radii and elbow bends differed, and neither exposed the seam between a rotated passage and the housing channel, so a grain could be pushed through a closed port or wedged against a boundary that the picture did not show. The previous step also had no deterministic fixtures: the only check sampled random layouts and reported a count.

Two modelling faults were confirmed before writing code. GLM's lateral air test `|lx| <= h || |ly| <= h` granted air across the whole cell beyond the channel mouth, and its rotor clip let air escape at the rim because the clip radius and the passage end were unrelated. Neither engine could say how wide an opening actually is at a given rotor angle.

## Changes

- Added `prototype/shared/geometry.js` as the single source of truth: seeded layout, rotor passage primitives (tube, tangent arc, chamber disk), housing corridors with pour and drain spouts, seam apertures, clearance query, contact-limited sweep, verified recovery and a stroke path built from the same primitives.
- Seam model: a passage is clipped at the rotor rim, and the opening it makes is the chord where the rotated passage still covers the housing cross-section, half width `(HALF - RV·|sin d|) / cos d`. It narrows continuously to zero as the rotor turns; inside the rotor the opening removes the rim wall, outside it limits what a grain can pass through. A shut seam leaves the rim as a wall for grains in the channel beside it.
- Clearance is the largest disc that fits, evaluated against the unions actually present: passages clipped by the rim, the seam opening, the chamber, and the housing corridors. No primitive is eroded independently.
- Movement is contact-limited: a sweep samples at half the grain radius and bisects the first blocked sample.
- A grain left in metal by wall motion is recovered by projecting onto each eroded primitive, keeping the nearest candidate that passes a clearance check. It is deterministic, never invents a position and reports how far it had to move.
- Corridors of neighbouring cells overlap by half a unit: the shared edge must not depend on which cell a point is assigned to, and an exact fit made the edge air depend on floating point.
- Added `prototype/shared/render.js` and the page `prototype/shared/index.html` with `app.js`: cached housings and rotor sprites drawn from the shared prims, exact black/white air for openings, fixed-step quarter-turn dwell that is repeatable per seed, pause, single step, speed, seed, shuffle and an air-model overlay that draws the clearance field a grain resolves.
- Housings are painted in three passes (dark edge, material, air) across the whole field, because neighbouring corridors cross at every cell edge and a strip's edge must not land on another strip's air.
- Added `experiments/geometry-fixtures.cjs` (geometry, seam, contact and recovery gates) and `experiments/render-smoke.cjs` (page wiring under a recording canvas and DOM stub).
- Recorded a timing baseline in `experiments/logs/geometry-0.1.1.txt`.

## Validation

- `node experiments/geometry-fixtures.cjs` passes on seeds 7, 11 and 1234: 300 valves, 27000 seam states, 27000 quarter-turn invariance comparisons, 7536 open openings and 40464 shut ones, 1200 housing corridors, 1080 shared cell edges, 5317 passage samples, 4390 no-invented-air probes, 6496 sweeps with 2885 contacts and 40464 shut-seam crossing attempts, 1727 exact clearance references (rotor centres, pocket depth, channel axes), 1098 recorded path commands, 1230 recovery cases and 1140 seam closures.
- Recovery matches a brute-force search on every sampled case (largest gap 0.00 px); a closing seam pushes a parked grain back by at most one grain radius (2.60 px) and never into the rotor.
- The sweep fixture compares each result with the first blocked sample of the same ray, so it fails both if a grain tunnels past a seam and if the sweep stops short.
- `node experiments/render-smoke.cjs` builds 100 sprites, strokes every passage three times, draws the background and each sprite, fills the air overlay and drives every control through the frame loop.
- `node experiments/geometry-check.cjs` still passes for the two prototypes.
- Static images of the page were rasterised once with a temporary local canvas package (`npm i --no-save`, not committed) and inspected against the air-model overlay: channels meet the rotor, openings align with drawn air, both air colors read correctly, pour spouts appear. This is a one-off visual check, not a test dependency.
- Not verified in a real browser: file-protocol loading, pointer and keyboard interaction, high-DPI behaviour and animation smoothness. The recording context and the rasteriser prove call order and static appearance only.

## Issues found and fixed while validating

- The housing corridor tapered to zero at the rotor rim, which closed the channel mouth: it now spans rim to cell edge, and the rim is a wall only where the rotor does not open it.
- Strip-local and valve-local coordinates were mixed in two places, caps were applied with `if` chains that dropped the larger value, and the arc stored an unnormalized end angle so a consumer interpolating `a0 -> a1` swept the long way.
- Passage caps were inverted: passages were walled off at the rim, where the disc clip already shapes the mouth, and open at the rotor centre, where a blind pocket needs a bottom.
- The seam opening was capped at the far side, which put a floating-point lip at every shared cell edge and could trap a grain passing between cells. The opening is now inside-only, and corridors overlap slightly.
- The renderer painted corridors in cell-local coordinates without translating to the cell centre, so every channel was drawn near the origin, and pour spouts were clipped out of the background sheet.
- Corridors painted strip by strip put dark edges on top of neighbouring air at every cell crossing; painting in passes fixed it.
- Half-turn symmetry and "a shut seam is air-free just outside the rim" were wrong expectations, not engine faults: the opening is an aperture at the rim and the channel is full width behind it.
- A zero-thickness wall means air can resume just beyond a contact, so anti-tunnelling must be asserted against the first blocked sample rather than a fixed probe distance.
- Fixtures must not assume the layout is unrotated after another fixture has turned the valves.

# Conservative grain simulation and review fixes

## Review of interrupted 0.2 work

Reviewing the partial `simulation.js`, `geometry.js`, `render.js` and `app.js` state exposed seven concrete bugs before extending the fixtures:

1. **Discontinuous seam-jaw clearance (`geometry.js`)**: outside the rim (`r >= RV`), `valveClearance` only applied the rim cap when `|lat| >= seam`. At `(lat = 0, r = RV + 0.05)` a narrowing seam reported `6.89 px` all the way down to `seam = 0.05 px` and collapsed to `0.05 px` on the next substep, letting grains sink into solid rim jaws and stalling valves. `nearestAir` had the matching `|cl| >= seam` gap.
2. **Double-counted rotor rotation and spinning chamber air (`simulation.js`)**: `carryWalls` rotated rotor grains by `omega * h` and `rotorStep` then stepped by world velocity (which already includes `omega × r`), spinning rotor grains at `2×` angular speed and dragging free-falling grains sideways in empty hollow-chamber air.
3. **Mid-air wall impulses on pile landings (`simulation.js`)**: pile bisection in `applyMove` set `sweepOut.hit = true` even when `G.sweep` hit no wall, firing `wallContact` along the distant wall gradient in mid-air.
4. **Step-0 stall stamp and infinite detent-stall loop (`simulation.js`)**: `stalledList` started at `0`, matching `sim.steps === 0` and undoing all 100 valves on step 0 and after `reset()`. On stall timeout past 45°, `Math.round(angle / HALF_PI)` re-selected the blocked forward detent forever; manual `turn()` also added `HALF_PI` to an in-flight angle instead of snapping to detents.
5. **Mouth scissor-pinch and order-dependent carry (`simulation.js`)**: `settleWalls` shoved mouth grains without checking neighbour overlap, and passive rotor carry across `r = RV` depended on grain index order, compressing trapped pairs to `> 2.8 px`.
6. **Static `drainReach` starving seeds (`geometry.js`, `simulation.js`)**: `drainReach` tested unrotated `angle = 0` port alignment instead of rotary connectivity (`ports.length > 0`), leaving seeds 12, 18 and 27 with `0` grains on both `seed()` and `pour()`, and seeding `0` upper hollow chambers.
7. **Inverted viewport translation and strobing overlay (`app.js`)**: `drawFrame` translated by `(0, -POUR)` instead of `(0, +POUR)` (cropping 60 px off the top), drew `airMap` at `(0, 0)` (30 px misaligned), and only blitted the overlay 1 frame in 8 so it strobed at 7.5 Hz. `resize` also rebuilt all 101 canvases even when DPR was unchanged.

## Changes

- `prototype/shared/simulation.js`: inertial world-frame velocity integration swept from passage-carried positions (central hollow cavities stay uncarried); wall velocity on rotor interiors and radial outer-rim contacts; multi-pass passive carry relaxation and mouth-pinch stall guard; `stalledList` initialized to `-1`, exact `prevAngle` undo and directional `floor`/`ceil` detent backoff; detent-snapped `turn()`; `BIN = 8` uniform grid; deterministic seeding of upper hollow reservoirs (`j < 3`) plus guaranteed active top pour spouts (`71–96` grains across tested seeds).
