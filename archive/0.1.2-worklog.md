# Browser feedback: presentation, sliding and editing

## Review

The prior solver correctly guarded walls and seams but discarded the rest of a step after first wall impact. Tangential velocity survived in state but could not advance along a supporting slope. Wall friction also added gravity support on top of the incoming normal gravity impulse. The outer background shadow radius left detached dark marks around the rotor. Width-only canvas sizing did not handle short landscape screens.

## Changes

- Swept remaining-time movement after wall response, guarded against particle overlap; lower wall friction independently of pile friction, lighter air damping. Physical grain radii and seam checks unchanged.
- GLM-style default: gradient screws, brighter rotor gradient, dark rim ticks, vivid grains. Combined and GPT-style skins selectable, sharing collision geometry; GPT uses lighter green-gray metal and no screws. Tightened background rim shadow for every skin.
- Grain drawing enlarged 16%, with soft offset shadows, without enlarging collision discs (intentional modest wall overlap for readability).
- Short landscape CSS uses a scrollable control rail and height-bounded field; portrait and normal desktop flow unchanged.
- Double-click replaces the valve type and purges only its cell, counted explicitly. Ctrl-click fills free rotor space with one random color. Replacement rebuilds geometry-derived reachability and render caches, resets that rotor's turn target, preserves other grains.
- Future pressure models specified separately in `pressure-plan.md`.

## Validation

- Simulation fixtures include downhill acceleration/inertia, replacement conservation, local purge, single-color fill and repeat-fill exclusion.
- Rotating-pocket angular acceptance now allows motion up to half a circle: lower friction changes the final grain location inside the same pocket. It still requires exact rotor detent, closed mouth, no recovery, no escape and conservation.
- Full seed-11 machine observation extended from 20 to 60 seconds: different trajectories change drain timing, so draining is not required within the old short window. Nine grains drain by 60 seconds; seed 12 drains four by 15 seconds. Max overlap 0.52 px, wall penetration 0.033 px, recovery push 0.05 px.
- Node render smoke covers all skins and Ctrl-click/double-click wiring. No real browser pixel/orientation testing available in this workspace; user browser verification remains necessary.
