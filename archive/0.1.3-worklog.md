# Browser feedback: grain liveliness, grain size, skin fidelity

## Review

The feedback round was reproduced with a new measurement script, `experiments/behavior-probe.cjs`, which runs the same five scenarios on the combined solver and on the GLM prototype engine as a behavioural reference:

- A two-grain vertical stack and a three-grain pyramid were **stable equilibria** in the combined solver (stack offset unchanged after 3 s), while GLM topples them in 0.13 s / 0.07 s.
- A column dropped through a pour spout stayed exactly where it landed (final spread 0.0 px): "it just falls and sits on that place".
- Inertial rolling along a flat floor covered 48 px before stopping, against ~95-240 px for GLM; elbow bends were exited but slowly.

Two root causes: `applyMove` bisects the whole displacement vector when the straight path dips into a contact circle, which scales the tangential (sliding) component to ~0 every substep; and pair friction 0.35 cancelled the small lateral impulses that tilted contacts produce. Grain readability came from radius: combined collided at 2.05-2.45 px drawn at 1.16×, while both prototypes use physical 2.2-3.1 px with real size variety.

Skin audit against rasterised pages: the combined "gpt" skin carried its own muted palette, three-band corridors, gradients and a wall band the prototype page never draws; the page paints flat tiles and metal from fixed colour lists, two-band corridors (`#46504f` edge, air), a `#354241` rim ring, rounded passage caps and pastel particles with a dot highlight. The GLM prototype drew its hub bolt on every rotor whose centre is solid, including blank plugs - the reported "filled valve without channels has central object like axis".

## Changes

- `prototype/shared/simulation.js`: when a pile contact blocks a move, the velocity is projected onto the contact tangent (normal of the deepest overlapping pair, recorded by `penetrationAt`) and the step is re-swept along it, so contacts block normally but never tangentially. Pair `FRICTION` 0.35 → 0.02, `WALL_FRICTION` 0.08 → 0.04, `DAMPING` 0.12 → 0.05: stacks and pyramids roll apart, columns spread, grains keep inertia on floors and slopes.
- `prototype/shared/simulation.js`: grain radii 2.3-3.1 px (default 2.7), pour-slot files/rows widened to match; `prototype/shared/render.js` draws bodies at radius + 0.2 with a per-skin grain style (shadow tint/offset, rim stroke, highlight) instead of a flat 1.16× inflation, keeping the intentional slight wall overlap.
- `prototype/shared/render.js`: the gpt skin now mirrors the prototype page exactly - grout/tile/metal colour lists, square tiles with the page's stroke, top highlight and right shade, two-band corridors, `#354241` rim ring, thin `#45504f` passage edges with no material band, rounded caps and joins, pastel particle palette and dot highlight; grain styles moved into a per-skin table.
- `prototype/glm-5.3-max/engine.js`: the hub bolt is drawn only where channels leave the centre solid (elbows); blank plugs keep a clean face.
- `experiments/behavior-probe.cjs` added; measurements recorded in `experiments/logs/simulation-0.1.3.txt`.

## Validation

- Behavior probe after the fix: stack topples at 0.34 s (GLM 0.13 s), pyramid at 0.63 s (GLM 0.07 s), column spread 54 px (was 0.0), roll 99 px (was 48 px), elbow exited true; GLM reference unchanged.
- `node experiments/simulation-fixtures.cjs`, `geometry-fixtures.cjs`, `render-smoke.cjs`, `geometry-check.cjs` all pass: falling column 6 drained, sealed reservoir 9 kept, quarter-turn elbow 2 rerouted, rotating pocket 2.70 rad carried, coincident split, conservation counters intact (69 seeded / 24 poured).
- 30 s machine runs: 3-20 grains drained per seed, recovered ≤ 3, wall penetration ≤ 0.010 px, transient pinch overlap ≤ 1.97 px (seed 3, was 0.85 px with the sticky pile), retained heap -3.2 KB; 1,000 turning grains cost 6.98 ms per 60 Hz frame on the reference box (was 5.13 ms; the tangential re-sweep adds one sweep and one penetration scan per blocked grain).
- Headless rasterisation with a temporary `@napi-rs/canvas` install (`npm i --no-save`, scratch script outside the repo, not committed) of all three combined skins and both prototype pages: gpt skin crops match the page's colours, bands, rim and grain paint; combined glm grains now read at prototype scale with varied sizes; GLM blank plugs show no hub.
- Not verified in a real browser: file-protocol loading, pointer interaction and on-screen animation smoothness still need the user's own screenshots.
