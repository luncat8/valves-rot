- **Single-sign bottle lifecycle**:
  - Signing an unsigned quest assigns `quest.owner`, spawns `1–2` player-tinted `'hollow single · direction ↑ N · 270° (ports N)'` goal bottles with explicit `GOAL_CHAMBER = 0.53 * RV` capacity, locks them out of background auto-rotation (`autoLocked[idx] = 1`), protects them from rival turns/brush overwrites (`goalOwner[idx] = playerId`), and ensures a gravity-reachable top spout is active in `spoutMask`.

- **Composable property-based quest generator & circular-mean hue evaluation**:
  - Composes count shape (`min..max` wide/narrow ranges, `N+` open counts, `1` or `7` exact counts), hue-band granularity (`any`, `2`-band Warm/Cool, `3`-band Ember/Verdant/Twilight, `6`-band single color), air-pressure targets (`> 0.68`/`0.78` high or `< 0.32`/`0.22` low), bottle count (`1` or `2`), and time limit (`none`, `75 s`, `45 s`) into `easy` / `med` / `hard` tiers and points.
  - Evaluates color-band quests by taking the max of direct in-band grain count and circular-mean hue match (`angleDiffDeg(meanHue, center) <= hueHalf` with resultant vector coherence `R >= 0.42`).
- **Bot AI & anti-corking placement**:
  - `pickGoalCells` spreads active bottles across distinct columns when available and never spawns a 1-port bottle directly above (`idx + N`) or below (`idx - N`) an existing goal bottle.
  - Grain bots run a zero-allocation backward BFS over gravity-valid drops and curved `elbow` deflectors, aligning chutes bottom-up (static cells before auto-rotating cells) and closing feeder valves above satisfied bounded/exact bottles; pressure bots orient their bottle toward the favorable boundary, sweep top/bottom vents, and avoid signing opposite-polarity pressure quests concurrently.

## Owned mouths and the drag dial (`0.2.1`)

### Problem
- Game mode exposed both ten-mouth bands to every player, so pressure play was a click grind: `20` markers per player across two bands, each a separate press, with nothing marking whose mouth was whose and no way to stop a rival from repainting the whole field from the bands.
- Press-drag turned a rotor after `33°` of orbit (`0.42 · π/2` snap) and ran a second, unrelated hub mode at `26 px` per detent. Small hand movement threw the target several detents, the two modes disagreed about sensitivity, and once the target latched there was no visual pull back, so the gesture felt jumpy and unclear.

### Owned mouths (Game mode)
- `inlets.js` gains `present`, `owner`, `PER_PLAYER = 2` and `deal(inlets, players, rnd)`: both bands are cleared, columns are shuffled, and each player is dealt **two columns**, each carrying one `in` (high source, bottom band) and one `out` (low vent, top band). `4 × players ≤ 10` columns, so no mouth is ever shared, and the in/out pair of a column is the pressure station a bottle in that column needs.
- `press(inlets, index, flip, actor)` mirrors `toggleInlet(sim, index, flip, actor)`: an owned mouth answers only to its owner and refuses the shift-click polarity flip; an empty slot is not a mouth. `canPress`/`ownedBy` back the cursor and the bot. `reset` restores the full sandbox bands.
- `game.js` owns the lifecycle: `dealMouths`/`restoreMouths` on `create`, `setMode` and `resetMatch` (a separate `mouthRandom` stream, so dealing never reshuffles the quests), `notice()` for "That mouth belongs to Ada.", `ownerMouthColumns()` for placement.
- Pressure quests now land in one of the **signer's own columns** (`scanOrder(..., ownOnly)`), so `ensureAirRoute`'s shaft ends at that player's in mouth below and out mouth above. `GAS.probeMouths(sim, wantHigh, column, owner)` solves the two extreme sets the signer can actually operate — their favourable pair open, their opposing pair sealed, other owners' mouths left alone — and that probe now uses the converged `steady` (the 12-iteration live cadence undershot a four-mouth set by up to `0.2` and made easy targets look unreachable). `fitPressureQuest` never asks for more headroom than the set reaches; the `±0.25` floor is kept only for a pocket no mouth moves.
- Rendering: `renderInlets` cases an owned mouth in the player colour (tinted disc, accent ring, arrow for its role, a `7 px` translucent stem down its column) and names it on hover (`You in`, `Ada out sealed`); `renderInletBadges` brackets it with owner plates on both band edges above the grain piles, so piles cannot hide it.
- Bots: the band-sweeping `botHoldAnchor`/`botFeedGoal`/`botChokeGoal`/`scanMouths` policy is replaced by `botMouthAction` — seal my opposing pair, open my favourable pair, then align the shaft.

### Drag dial
- `dragPull()` is the single mapping: the orbital hand owes `π/2` of travel per detent with `0.22 rad` of grip slack and a half-detent snap, so the first detent needs `1.01 rad` (`58°`) of orbit where the old asymmetric snap fired at `0.91 rad` — a hair less twitchy and symmetric in both directions; the hub pull costs `52 px` per detent after `9 px` of slack; `6 px` still separates a click from a drag. The mode is latched where the press lands (`r0 < 12 px` = hub), so it cannot flip halfway through a gesture.
- `renderDrag` draws the dial around a fixed `RV + 4.6` ring: faint ring and four detent ticks, a `3 px` arc growing with the hand pull (not the latched target), a bold notch on the committed detent, and a dot where the hand grips the rim; the hub pull gets its own horizontal rail with one tick per `52 px` and a knob under the hand. The cursor reads `grab`, `grabbing`, `pointer` or `not-allowed` from what is under the pointer.
