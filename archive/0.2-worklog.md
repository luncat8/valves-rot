## Pre-`v0.2` Controls & Player-Tinted Housings
- **Auto-rotate `%` slider (`#auto-rotate`)**:
  - Added deterministic seeded Fisher–Yates permutation `sim.autoRank` (`Uint8Array(100)`), `sim.autoLocked`, and `S.setAutoRotate(sim, fraction)` in `simulation.js`.
  - Defaults to `90%` in **Lab** mode and `20%` in **Game** mode (`S.create` defaults to `100%` when omitted so historical fixtures remain unchanged).
  - Dropping the slider snaps newly excluded mid-flight valves to their nearest 90° detent (`S.setTarget`).
- **Press-drag valve rotation (`app.js` + `render.js`)**:
  - Orbital drag (`r >= 10 px` from valve center) steps `sim.target` in 90° detents following `atan2(wy - cy, wx - cx)` delta; center-hub horizontal pull (`r < 10 px`) steps 1 quarter-turn per `24 px` of horizontal travel.
  - Added `R.renderDrag` guide arc/tick overlay and `suppressClick` guard so pointer release after dragging never triggers a duplicate click turn.
- **Player-colored housing tints (`sim.caseTint` + `R.PLAYERS`)**:
  - Added 4 high-contrast player palettes (`0` **You** Gold, `1` **Ada** Coral, `2` **Bolt** Cyan, `3` **Lyra** Violet) painted into recessed valve housings across all three skins (`glm`, `combined`, `gpt`), plus a **Case tint** brush row in the **Place valve** drawer for Lab experimentation.

## `v0.2` Game Mode, Quests & Bot Competitors (`prototype/shared/game.js`)
- **Mode & Competitors UI**:
  - Added **Mode** rail switcher (`Lab` / `Game`, shortcut `G`) and a side/stacked `#game-panel` with **Bot control** checkbox, **New match** button, 4-player scoreboard, and 5-card **Goals** board.
- **Single-sign bottle lifecycle**:
  - Signing an unsigned quest assigns `quest.owner`, spawns `1–2` player-tinted `'hollow single · direction ↑ N · 270° (ports N)'` goal bottles with explicit `GOAL_CHAMBER = 0.53 * RV` capacity, locks them out of background auto-rotation (`autoLocked[idx] = 1`), protects them from rival turns/brush overwrites (`goalOwner[idx] = playerId`), and ensures a gravity-reachable top spout is active in `spoutMask`.
  - Satisfying all bottle conditions across the hold window (`0.45 s` for open `N+`, `1.0 s` for bounded/exact/pressure) awards points, replaces each goal bottle with `'solid blank'` (purging contained grains cleanly via `clearValve`), clears case tints, removes the completed card, and spawns a replacement quest.
- **Composable property-based quest generator & circular-mean hue evaluation**:
  - Composes count shape (`min..max` wide/narrow ranges, `N+` open counts, `1` or `7` exact counts), hue-band granularity (`any`, `2`-band Warm/Cool, `3`-band Ember/Verdant/Twilight, `6`-band single color), air-pressure targets (`> 0.68`/`0.78` high or `< 0.32`/`0.22` low), bottle count (`1` or `2`), and time limit (`none`, `75 s`, `45 s`) into `easy` / `med` / `hard` tiers and points.
  - Evaluates color-band quests by taking the max of direct in-band grain count and circular-mean hue match (`angleDiffDeg(meanHue, center) <= hueHalf` with resultant vector coherence `R >= 0.42`).
- **Bot AI & anti-corking placement**:
  - `pickGoalCells` spreads active bottles across distinct columns when available and never spawns a 1-port bottle directly above (`idx + N`) or below (`idx - N`) an existing goal bottle.
  - Grain bots run a zero-allocation backward BFS over gravity-valid drops and curved `elbow` deflectors, aligning chutes bottom-up (static cells before auto-rotating cells) and closing feeder valves above satisfied bounded/exact bottles; pressure bots orient their bottle toward the favorable boundary, sweep top/bottom vents, and avoid signing opposite-polarity pressure quests concurrently.
