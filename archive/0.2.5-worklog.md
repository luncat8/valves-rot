# Unlinked mouths, turn-based vent rotation, and bot pacing (0.2.5)

## Review

The previous batch left two loose ends that this one closes. Vent mode still drew
its four dealt mouths per seat identically, so a fallback mouth with no route to
the collector looked exactly like a routed one. Valve play was refused outright
in Vent mode (`canTurn` returned `false` for every cell), and bots acted on a
random `0.48–0.9 s` cooldown that ignored who was playing. `archive/vent-worklog.md`
had also recorded `f237a20` as unresolved.

## Implementation

- **Inert mouths read as owner marks.** `ValveInlets` now carries a `linked`
  flag: `1` for a mouth that opens onto a route, `0` for a fallback boundary
  hole. Vent setup stores the flag per pair (`seat.inLinked`, `seat.outLinked`)
  and marks the dealt mouths, so a mouth the branch search could not join is
  drawn as a player-accent ring only — no stem, no flow arrow, and no
  field-edge owner plate. Its hover label adds `· no route`. The mouth stays
  pressable: it is still a real boundary hole, it just cannot steer the
  collector.
- **Turn-based valve rotation in Vent mode.** Seats take turns in order; the
  seat on turn may rotate exactly one unlocked valve by 90° (a click or
  <kbd>Enter</kbd>, `Shift` for the other way). The rotation spends the turn at
  once, a one second hand-off follows, and the turn moves to the next seat with a
  collector. A turn left unspent expires after `10 s`, and a **Pass** button in
  the seat's panel row hands it on. Route cells and collectors stay locked
  (`autoLocked`/`goalOwner`), so a prebuilt path cannot be dismantled; the
  press-drag dial is off in this mode because it would buy several detents per
  turn. The seat on turn wears a second ring on its collector.
- **A bot spends or passes its turn by itself.** It waits `1.6 s`, then rotates a
  random unlocked valve in the same gas-network component as its own collector
  only while its band is slipping; otherwise it passes. Paused bot control still
  hands the turn on, so a person never waits behind a seat nobody plays.
- **Bot pacing by difficulty.** Every seat carries a level (`Calm 1 Hz`,
  `Brisk 1.5 Hz`, `Quick 2 Hz`) chosen from a select in the roster row. One
  action per `1 / rate` interval (jitter only slows it) is the ceiling, and the
  first action already honours it, so a long frame still buys a single map
  change. Game mode's `stepBots` uses that interval instead of the old random
  cooldown.

## Branch `old` / `f237a20`

`f237a20` ("Vent mode correction") is the tip of branch `old`. It and current
`main`'s `db61c7e` ("Vent mode: random-map pressure regulation") are siblings
after the same parent `4dd8c5b`; `old` was committed at 03:04Z and `main` at
04:06Z on 2026-10-30. Diffing them shows `old` holds the *earlier* Vent pass:
one source/vent mouth per seat instead of two, no route-cell reservation for a
later seat, no lock preservation for shared route cells, no `autoLocked` on
planned paths, and the superseded compact vent row. `main` keeps every one of
those improvements and adds the four-mouth deal. Nothing in `old` is missing
from `main`, so the branch is a leftover savepoint from the rewrite, not a
parallel design worth merging. It is safe to delete; history alone cannot prove
intent, so it was left in place.

## Validation

- `node experiments/vent-fixtures.cjs`: linked/unlinked mouth flags, one
  rotation per turn, route/collector lock refusal, the hand-off clock, the idle
  turn expiring, a bot turning while out of band, pass, and a fresh turn on
  re-entry.
- `node experiments/bot-pacing.cjs`: measured 40 s match — `1 Hz` seat gaps
  `1.01–1.17 s`, `1.5 Hz` `0.67–0.79 s`, `2 Hz` `0.51–0.59 s`, quick seat acts
  more often than calm, and a person's seat keeps no bot clock.
- `node experiments/render-smoke.cjs`: roster carries `data-level`, vent panel
  names the seat on turn, a collector click is refused as a locked route cell,
  an unlocked valve turns once and the second click on the same turn is refused.
- `node experiments/vent-balance.cjs`: four seeds, one and two seats, exact
  restore. `gas-fixtures`, `air-fixtures`, `game-fixtures`,
  `pressure-propagation-fixtures`, `simulation-fixtures`, `geometry-fixtures`,
  `geometry-check` and `calibration-fixtures` still pass.
- Rendered the Vent field offscreen (`@napi-rs/canvas`, ad hoc) and sampled the
  band pixels: a linked mouth tints its column stem with the owner accent, an
  unlinked mouth leaves the pipe white.

## Known open item

Measuring the turn loop turned up a pre-existing vent balance bug that also
reproduces on `main`: after the first scored band the next band can be fitted
outside what the seat's pocket can reach. One bot seat on seed 7 holds and scores
`0.41–0.57`, then gets `0.59–0.75` while the pocket sits at `0.538` and does not
move with both in mouths open and both out mouths sealed — `7%` held and one
round per minute. The turn is the first lever that can open a pocket further, so
a seat that has been charging without progress for a few seconds could spend its
turn on a valve next to its collector instead of passing.
