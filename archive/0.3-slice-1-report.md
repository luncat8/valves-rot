## Plan review — what changed

The core decisions held up (dominant-normalized `255·v/max(v)`, three pour boxes, fixed-1000 miscible phase, per-layer buoyancy). Six things were vague or wrong enough to fix before writing code:

- **One conserved state.** Channels (`ch`, stride 3) are authoritative, `vol` is their cache with a *published residual*; every transfer bottoms out in one `move(area)` that splits proportionally and gives the last channel the remainder so a move is exact. Otherwise "proportional transfer" has to be re-implemented on eight paths (push, trapped return, supply, overflow, drain…) and one will lose a colour.
- **Phases are derived, not stored.** A phase is a sum of channels under the mixability partition, so split/merge on a checkbox toggle rewrites nothing — "repeated toggles must not lose volume" becomes structural instead of tested.
- **Green ≠ green.** Green liquid at 1000 is the neutral *teal* grain's density, not the 800 green grain; the plan now says the liquids are named by hue, the densities by family.
- **Per-phase displacement** is defined (charge a grain's submerged area to the band holding its submerged centroid, clamp at zero) instead of left open.
- **Display cost/allocation** pinned down: one byte triple per *water cell* per frame, addressed by precomputed row/column offsets; pigment stays a pure testable function.
- **Slice 1 split** into a state half and a view half, and the four existing fixtures that poke `w.vol` are named for migration.

## Implemented (slice 1)

`water.js`: channel array + `trappedCh`, `move`/`deposit`, `setLiquid`/`inject` (an imposed area is an *opening-inventory* term so the ledger closes), `takeAll`, trap/release, per-channel books beside the existing totals, `setPour` (equal shares, refuses an empty mask), `setMixable`, `channelTotals`, `residual`. All eight transfer paths rewired; no direct `vol` write survives.

Views: colour and pigment fill sharing the existing depth alpha, `R.pigmentLevel` exported for the anchors; page gained R/G/B pour boxes, a Color/Pigment radio and a per-channel share readout.

One thing the plan missed: with all three colours pouring, the normalized triple is **white**, and white over pale porcelain made the pool vanish (see `experiments/logs/water-state-0.3.png`). The colour view now paints the triple at a fixed 55% shade, with the field's triple left unshaded so the mapping and pigment anchors stay exact.
