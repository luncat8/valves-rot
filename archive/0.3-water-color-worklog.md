## Slice 1 — review, channel state, books, pour shares and both views

- Reviewed the plan against the code before implementing. Improvements merged into
  `0.3-water-color-plan.md`: channel volumes are the only conserved state and `vol`
  is their cache with a published residual; one `move()`/`deposit()` pair carries
  every transfer, with the last channel taking the remainder so an area moves
  exactly; phases are derived from the mixability flags rather than stored, so a
  toggle cannot lose volume and split/merge is bit-neutral by construction; green
  liquid at 1,000 is named as the neutral *teal* grain's density, not the 800 green
  grain; per-phase displacement is charged to the band holding a grain's submerged
  centroid (documented approximation); the display computes one byte triple per
  water cell and the texel loop copies it; slice 1 split into a state half and a
  view half so the ledger is testable before any pixel changes.
- Implemented `water.js`: `ch` (stride 3) + `trappedCh`, `move`, `deposit`,
  `setLiquid`/`inject` (an imposed area is an opening-inventory term, so the ledger
  closes), `takeAll`, `trap`/`releaseTrapped`, per-channel books beside the
  existing totals, `setPour` (equal shares, refuses an empty mask),
  `setMixable` (flags only), `channelTotals`, `residual`, and a per-water-cell
  normalized colour table in the display field. All eight transfer paths now end
  in the one primitive; no direct `vol` write survives (grep).
- Implemented the views: `renderWater(map, field, view)` paints the shaded channel
  triple (colour) or `L = clamp((765 - sum)/2, 0, 255)` (pigment), sharing the
  existing depth alpha, and `R.pigmentLevel` exposes the mapping for fixtures. The
  page gained R/G/B pour boxes that re-sync from the module when a selection is
  refused, a Color/Pigment radio pair, and a per-channel share readout in the
  status line.
- Measured a legibility problem the plan did not cover: with all three colours
  pouring, the normalized triple is white, and white over the pale porcelain
  vanished (`experiments/logs/water-state-0.3.webp`). Fixed by painting the colour
  view at a fixed 55% shade while leaving the field's triple unshaded, and recorded
  the convention in the plan, Help and README.
- Validation: new `experiments/water-color-fixtures.cjs` (11 cases) — per-channel
  ledger through supply, overflow, drain, reset, split/merge and trapped reopen;
  `vol` residual; proportional moves; pour selection changing mix but not volume;
  normalized and shaded display bytes; pigment anchors 255/128/0; view toggling
  leaving a channel snapshot identical; mixability toggles touching nothing; 20 s
  closed box (residual 1.4e-14 px², held 3.3e3 px²). Existing fixtures migrated to
  `setLiquid`/`inject` and re-run green: `water-fixtures`, `water-state-fixtures`,
  `water-buoyancy-fixtures`, `water-separation` (100% both groups, 0%
  contamination), `water-balance` (wander 2.25 px at drag 6), `water-gate` (4/4
  floats, 6/6 dense, 0 strayed), `water-picture`, `render-smoke` (now also asserts
  the pour boxes and the view toggle), plus air/gas/vent/game/geometry/calibration.
- Timing on the recorded machine, same bench on `2f7c8c3` and on this slice:
  wet static 0.129 → 0.138 ms/step, wet turning 0.695 → 0.704, 16 ms mask rebuild
  11.3, display pass 0.98 → 1.05–1.17 ms. No new allocation in the step or render
  loop.
- Next step: slice 2 (separated phases) — density order from the mixability flags,

  to a phase's band, per-phase published boundaries and band-summed buoyancy, with
  the red-under-green-under-blue settling budget and the all-mixable baseline
