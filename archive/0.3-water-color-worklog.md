## Slice 2: separated phases

- Derived phases from the mixability flags (checked channels share one phase at
  1000 kg/m³; unchecked R/G/B stand alone at 2000/1000/500), with partition,
  order and densities sorted once per flag change into preallocated scratch.
  Channel volume is untouched by toggling: repeated splits and merges are
  bit-neutral by construction, and the all-mixable solver stays the
  single-liquid path behind a `phaseCount < 2` fast lane.
- Sorting is two passes inside the face loop. Vertical neighbours swap equal
  slice areas heavy-down/light-up, capped by both availabilities and a capacity
  fraction, keeping each cell's total so the published surface never moves with
  a swap. That alone freezes in the lock-exchange setup (heavy beside light at
  one surface reads as settled), so horizontal neighbours trade heavy for light
  down the lateral mean-density gradient in equal areas with an exact
  non-crossing cap; equal-density phases never trade. Sill spills walk the
  source's phases top-down against one budget; trapped liquid keeps its
  composition and sorts on reopening.
- Published per-cell phase bands (dense-first boundaries) next to the surface.
  Grain buoyancy sums `g·(1 − Σ ρ_layer/ρ_grain·f_layer)` over the bands the
  disc crosses, with band fractions as differences of the circular-segment
  kernel; drag still uses the total wetted fraction. A below-surface cell that
  empties inherits bands and level from below, or buoyancy reads NaN.
- The display field carries up to three dense-first triples per cell with band
  boundaries; each water texel walks at most two boundaries to its band, then
  paints at the slice-1 55% shade with the existing depth alpha. The page
  gained Mix R/G/B boxes (shared densities with the grain palette, so a blue
  grain hangs neutral in blue liquid) and Help documents the layers.
- Validation: new `experiments/water-color-phase.cjs` (9 cases, green in
  ~2.6 s) — inverted R/G/B stack orders in 5 s (tube; coms 625/599/551,
  residue 1.1e-6 px²) and a wide basin in 10 s with drift under 1e-3 px over
  5 s more; blue poured under red reaches the top half in 5 s; a 2:1 R+G mix
  holds its ratio to 2e-16 under a blue layer; a shut gate passes 0.0000 px²
  over 5 s and holds bit-exact; split/merge carries 1 px² and returns every
  channel; one-step grain accelerations match the band sum to 6e-7 (−g, +g/2,

## Slice 3, scenarios and measurement

- Reviewed slice 2's transfer and phase fixtures. A longer closed-box run found
  tiny negative channel values (minimum -6.78e-21 px²) under repeated partition
  changes. The cause is the split itself, not the ledger: every channel but one
  moves `channel * (area / total)` — a product with a factor below one, which can
  never exceed its own channel — and the remainder went to the LAST channel. The
  last channel can be empty, and the remainder is a rounding error whose size is
  set by `area`, so a dust-sized move against a large cell charged a positive
  share to a zero channel and left it negative. Instrumenting `move` and
  `movePhase` found 18 and 70 such overdraws in the 60 s toggle run, at dust
  magnitudes from 1e-36 to 2.5e-14 px².
- Fix: the remainder goes to the LARGEST channel of the triple (or of the phase),
  which holds at least a third of the move and therefore sits far from both
  bounds; each share is clamped to its own channel; an emptying transfer moves
  every channel exactly; and the cell cache advances by what actually moved
  instead of by the requested area. Nonnegativity is now a property of the split
  rather than a tolerance. The all-mixable golden stayed bit-identical: the
  `--record` digest before and after the change diffs clean, including the
  distribution checksum and the three grain positions.
- Two regression cases that fail on the old rule and pass on the new one: `move`
  of 3.6e-4 px² out of a 27 / 9 / 0 cell (overdrew blue by 1.4e-20) in
  `water-color-fixtures.cjs`, and `movePhase` of 1.89e-3 px² out of a 27 / 0 / 9
  cell with R+G mixed (overdrew green by 2.2e-19) in `water-color-phase.cjs`.
  Both were verified to fail against the previous `water.js`.
- `experiments/water-color-balance.cjs` now scans all 46800 channel values after
  EVERY step, not once per second, and asserts the true minimum. Seed 3, 120 Hz,
  60 s, 60 toggles: ledger 9.1e-13 px² absolute / 6.1e-16 relative, cell cache
  4.1e-13 px², minimum channel 0.0 px² over 7200 steps, no negative scan, no flow
  across the closed boundary, and every toggle bit-neutral on channels, trapped
  shares and totals. Runtime 2.5 s; the per-step scan is ~2% of the loop.
