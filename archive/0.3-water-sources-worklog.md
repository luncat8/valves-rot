# 0.3 water sources worklog

## Review

Read the requested draft against the current water code. The single global
inlet (one `ventColumn`, one `ventOpen`, one `rate`, one `pour`) had no canvas
presence at all: the pour was aimed by a slider and marked by nothing on the
field. The plan (`0.3-water-sources-plan.md`) kept the existing fixture API
alive by making the three scalars a mirror of a "current vent" instead of
removing them, which also matched the request's "global column settings cause
set parameters of current vent".

## Implementation

- `geometry.js`: `VENT_STRIP = 16`, the control strip above the pour band.
- `water.js`: per-column `ventOn` / `ventRate` / `ventShares` plus
  `ventVersion` and a published `rateMax`; `supply` loops the vents and
  deposits each one's own rate and shares at `mouthCell(w, column)` through the
  same books; `syncCurrent` keeps the scalar mirror on `ventColumn` true after
  every mutation; `setVent` selects and sets the flag, `selectVent` selects,
  `setSupply`/`setPour` edit the current vent, `setVentRate` /
  `setVentShares` / `toggleVent` edit one vent directly (black shares refused,
  rate capped at `RATE_MAX`), `sweepVents` stamps the departure vent onto
  every vent it passes, `ventOpenCount` for the status line;
  `hueShares`/`sharesHue` map the RGB wheel to and from share triples over the
  six sector corners; `reset` shuts every vent and keeps their parameters.
- `inlets.js`: `hit` claims only the band itself, so the strip above it
  belongs to the water vents.
- `render.js`: background sheet, frame clear and canvas grow by the strip
  (air/water overlays unchanged); `renderWaterVents` draws one tab per column
  — colour chip, rate gauge, sealed cross, current outline, hover/drag label —
  and turns the tab into a hue spectrum with a cursor during a left/right
  slide. Colour strings are cached per vent and rebuilt only on a
  `ventVersion` bump, so the frame loop allocates none.
- `app.js`: the vent gesture machine (`ventDrag`) — a press arms after 6 px
  and locks its axis to the first dominant movement (one tab width = one hue
  revolution, 60 px of vertical travel = the whole rate range), a hold past
  480 ms toggles and suppresses the trailing click, a double-click opens the
  hidden native colour input prefilled with the vent's normalized colour, and
  a plain click selects. Every gesture makes its vent current. The Column
  slider now sweeps: `sweepVents` from the last shown value, so dragging it
  paints and a jump stamps the span. `syncWaterUI` mirrors the current vent
  and keeps `panelColumn` true; the stats line reports `vents N open`.
- `index.html` / `style.css`: the colour input (rendered but out of sight, so
  `showPicker` accepts it), a one-line gesture hint in the Water fieldset, a
  full Water vents paragraph plus five shortcut rows in Help, and the canvas
  aria-label describes the gestures.

## Validation

- New `experiments/water-sources-fixtures.cjs` (7 cases), all green: two vents
  pour pure red and pure blue into their own columns at a measured 1:2 rate
  ratio with the channel ledger closed; the single-vent API still pours
  exactly one column (26 px² stored, the rest drains through the open pipe);
  a shut N spout confines its own vent while the neighbour pours; hue corners
  exact, ramps linear, ±wrap, grey reports 0; per-vent edits land while the
  mirror follows the current vent and refusals change nothing; the sweep
  stamps vents 2–4 from vent 1, skips vent 8, and a 1→9 jump turns the row
  red; reset shuts all and keeps rates and colours.
- Migrated two fixtures whose `setPour` preceded their `setVent`
  (`water-color-fixtures.cjs` case 11, `water-gate.cjs` `pourTo`): shares are
  per vent, so the vent is selected first. `water-gate` re-measured its
  published numbers: 4/4 floats captured, 6/6 dense below, 0 strayed, 0 lost.
- Full re-run green: `water-fixtures`, `water-state-fixtures`,
  `water-buoyancy-fixtures`, `water-color-fixtures`, `water-color-phase`,
  `water-color-balance`, `water-mixed-baseline`, `water-separation`,
  `water-balance`, `water-gate`, `water-scenario`, `water-paint`,
  `water-picture`, `water-color-picture`, `water-color-scenario`,
  `geometry-check`, `geometry-fixtures`, `simulation-fixtures`,
  `gas-fixtures`, `air-fixtures`, `pressure-propagation-fixtures`,
  `calibration-fixtures`, `game-fixtures`, `vent-fixtures`, `vent-balance`,
  `bot-pacing`, `game-balance`, `packed-flow`, `behavior-probe`,
  `front-probe`, `aperture-probe`, `simulation-bench`.
- `render-smoke.cjs` extended and green: the stub rect matches the taller
  canvas, the root transform is +POUR +VENT_STRIP, the background sheet covers
  the strip while the air/gas overlays keep their rects, band clicks moved
  below the strip, and new cases drive a vent click (select), a hue slide
  (white → 180° cyan through the pour boxes), a rate slide (+3000 px²/s), the
  picker prefill and a picked orange, and a swept vent read back open at the
  stamped rate. The stub's `water-column` default now mirrors the HTML's "6"
  so the water-on flow starts where the page does.
- `water-color-bench` on this machine: wet static 0.255/0.284 ms, turning
  0.681/0.724, rebuild 8.11 — the per-vent supply loop costs nothing
  measurable at 120 Hz.
- Headless-browser pass on the real page (synthetic pointer events): the
  long-press toggles (stats line `vents 1 open` with white water pouring), a
  one-tab-width hue slide turns the current vent cyan (pour boxes G+B), a
  30 px up-slide adds exactly +3000 px²/s, the picker prefills the vent's
  normalized colour and a pink pick lands R+B, the column sweep stamps a shut
  pink vent onto its neighbours (read back by clicking them), and canvas pixel
  probes read the chips: pure cyan on the open vent, dimmed white defaults,
  dimmed pink on the swept vents. A vision pass over the screenshot confirms
  the strip renders cleanly with no artifacts.
- Found and fixed while driving synthetic gestures: `setPointerCapture` throws
  `NotFoundError` for a pointer id that is not active, which killed the arming
  move's apply. Real pointers are always active during their own events, but
  the arm is now guarded like the release already was.
- The long-press timing itself is not covered headlessly (real timers); the
  toggle it triggers is covered at the module level by `toggleVent` in the new
  fixtures.
