## Heat field foundation review

- Reviewed the current heat-field foundation and its fixture before extending into material coupling. The implementation already covered enthalpy, derived temperature, internal conduction, valve sources, ambient exchange, a substep bound, geometry rebuild and reset; its parameter setter did not reject non-finite/negative capacities or conductances, and zero conductance produced an infinite intermediate stability ratio. Added atomic validation for positive finite capacities/mass, nonnegative finite conductances, and an integer substep budget; the zero-conductance case now explicitly requires one pass.
- The existing rotor fixture asserted bit-exact stored energy after a full simulation step. A conduction pass may redistribute enthalpy with a tiny summation residual, so the test now checks conservation at a scale-relative floating-point tolerance instead of conflating roundoff with a geometry leak. Timestep refinement was also strengthened: compare 1/120, 1/240 and 1/480 and verify the error decreases when the timestep halves, rather than requiring two finite-step solutions to match to 1e-3 °C.
- Tightened the plan's boundary between the heat-field foundation and material coupling/view work. The first slice explicitly excludes material advection; the next slice owns grain/liquid enthalpy transport and the temperature view. This makes the currently executable acceptance gate smaller and prevents an energy ledger from silently claiming to cover material that is not yet coupled.
- Review exposed a stale script list in `experiments/render-smoke.cjs`: `simulation.js` now depends on `heat.js`, but the VM smoke harness did not load it. Added `heat.js` before `simulation.js`; the smoke then passes without changing runtime behavior.
- Validation: `node experiments/heat-fixtures.cjs`, `node experiments/simulation-fixtures.cjs`, and `node experiments/render-smoke.cjs` PASS; `node --check` for heat and the reviewed color-balance test PASS; `git diff --check` PASS. In the browser, `simulation.js` therefore evaluated `typeof ValveHeat !== "undefined"` as false and called `require("./heat.js")`, producing `Uncaught ReferenceError: require is not defined` at simulation.js:19. The aborted script left `ValveSimulation` uninitialized, so `game.js` then reported the misleading follow-on `ValveSimulation is not defined`. Added `heat.js` before `simulation.js`.

It now reads the `<script src>` list from `index.html` and `air-scale.html` and asserts that every `require("./x.js")` fallback is preceded by `x.js`. Removing `heat.js` from the page makes the guard fail with `simulation.js needs heat.js`.
