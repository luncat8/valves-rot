## Heat field foundation review

- Reviewed the current heat-field foundation and its fixture before extending into material coupling. The implementation already covered enthalpy, derived temperature, internal conduction, valve sources, ambient exchange, a substep bound, geometry rebuild and reset; its parameter setter did not reject non-finite/negative capacities or conductances, and zero conductance produced an infinite intermediate stability ratio. Added atomic validation for positive finite capacities/mass, nonnegative finite conductances, and an integer substep budget; the zero-conductance case now explicitly requires one pass.
- The existing rotor fixture asserted bit-exact stored energy after a full simulation step. A conduction pass may redistribute enthalpy with a tiny summation residual, so the test now checks conservation at a scale-relative floating-point tolerance instead of conflating roundoff with a geometry leak. Timestep refinement was also strengthened: compare 1/120, 1/240 and 1/480 and verify the error decreases when the timestep halves, rather than requiring two finite-step solutions to match to 1e-3 °C.

The first slice explicitly excludes material advection; the next slice owns grain/liquid enthalpy transport and the temperature view. This makes the currently executable acceptance gate smaller and prevents an energy ledger from silently claiming to cover material that is not yet coupled.

## Slice 1b — material coupling and temperature view

- Reviewed slice 1a before extending it. Two gaps found and fixed: `heat.step` keyed its mask resample on `w.serial` only, so a heat field created before the water grid's first build kept its zero-sample capacity and all-metal conductance until some rotor turned — the check now includes `w.built`; and `heat.reset` zeroed the per-valve powers, which disagreed with the water reset's keep-rates-and-colours convention — powers and parameters now survive reset, matching the plan's persistence wording.
- Material coupling: water gained nullable `onFlow`/`onEnter`/`onLeave` hooks fired inside `move`, `movePhase` (restructured to compute its per-channel amounts before mutating, through a dedicated `mvScratch`), `takeAll`/`drainAll` and the vent supply; simulation gained a per-grain `gcell` in `GRAIN_FIELDS`, `removeGrains` (batched compaction for melts), `slotFree` export, and `grainGone` calls in compact/clearValve/drain. The latent book rides the material books from this slice on (supply enters with sensible plus latent), so slice 3 did not have to rewrite the hooks.
- The first pricing design (parcels at the donor's last published temperature) failed its own fixture: the relaxation sloshes one parcel up and back within a step, so a cell whose composition drifted priced its exports off a stale temperature, the flow guard clamped, cold parcels reached the pour band and the whole band froze into a storm of pinned 120/100/80 cells. Fix: `liveT(i)` — the same derived-temperature formula computed from the *current* composition — prices every parcel, export and grain crossing. The pour then stays at its ambient import price to within ~0.1 E/px² of throughput, and no plateau engages.
- Also fixed while debugging: the melt room check counted the melting grain's own `gdisp` displacement, so a grain in a wet cell could never melt — its own occupancy blocked it — which is what let the pinned cells pile energy without bound. The grain's displacement no longer counts against its own melt's room.
- View and controls: `renderHeat` (one texel per water cell through a fixed 0–240 °C LUT), `renderHeatLegend` (shares the pressure legend slot; the air backends are parked while water is on), `renderHeatBadges` (heater/cooler discs with arrow glyphs), and `renderWater` gained a `dim` factor (0.42 while the temperature view is on). The Heat fieldset (on/off, signed power for the selected valve, ambient, view toggle, readout) is wired in app.js; the status line carries mean °C plus the four ledger terms and the residual.
- Validation: `node experiments/heat-fixtures.cjs` — slice 1a gates 1–7 unchanged and green; new gates 8–14: liquid movement conserves the books through a two-chamber exchange, supply/overflow/drain close the material book within the live-pricing wiggle bound (2e-3 of throughput), grain crossings import at ambient and export at the cell's temperature, a rotor turn with liquid and a grain keeps the field's energy, all 15,600 overlay texels equal the published temperature's LUT color with both clamp ends, disable freezes the books while water runs and re-enable resumes, and the step cost is 1.72–1.74× the heat-off baseline on the same wet scene (0.85 → 1.46 ms/step). Water, water-state, water-colour, water-buoyancy, water-sources, simulation, game, vent, gas, geometry, calibration, pressure-propagation and air fixtures all PASS.

## Post-release fixes — game-mode crash and the dry-crossing runaway

- Bug (Game mode): switching Lab → Game with water on crashed the page (`Cannot read properties of null (reading 'net')` in `gas.js` rebuild, via `signQuest` → `GAS.refresh`). `setPressure` refuses a non-off mode while water is enabled — the two are exclusive — but `setMode`, `signQuest`, `buildVent` and `botStepPressureQuest` all ignored the failure and dereferenced the null `sim.pressure`. Fix: `setMode` parks the water field (and with it heat) before dealing mouths or building vent routes — the same remember-and-restore contract `setWater` already keeps for the pressure mode; the parked field survives and the Lab panel's checkbox re-enables it. The four `setPressure` call sites in game.js now refuse the action instead of proceeding on a null backend, and the Lab-only water/heat checkboxes disable outside Lab mode (`syncWaterUI`/`syncHeatUI` gated on `game.mode`, `applyPlayMode` syncs heat too), so the panel cannot re-break a running match.
- Bug (Lab heat): the field ran away to ~1e59 E within seconds of opening a water vent (ledger residual ~1e47, melted/frozen grams nonsensical; reproduced on default layouts — seeds 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 11, 42 blow up, 1 and 100 hold). `grainsScan` zeroed the per-cell grain capacity `CG` before pricing the crossings, so a grain leaving a dry cell was priced at `liveT` over a `DRY_CAP`-floored capacity; the flow guard clamped the parcel to the whole donor and one grain carried the entire cell across — the receiver pinned at its melt plateau, the donor collapsed to ~0 °C, and the storm fed itself. A single falling grain never trips it (the clamp then moves exactly its own share), which is why the grain-crossing gate stayed green. Fix: two passes — crossings first against last step's `CG`, then the `CG`/`minMelt` rebuild. All nine seeds hold their books at ≤1e-9 relative for 3000 steps after the fix.
- Regression gates: heat fixture 14 (a five-grain dry column; one grain teleported into the upper chamber's partially-open cell moves exactly its sensible share and both cells keep the uniform 60 °C — on the old code the donor gives the whole cell, 371.28 vs 190.44 E, and the receiver pins at 80 °C) and game fixture `testWaterPark` (Lab water parks on Game/Vent entry, a pressure quest signs over the grid gas, Lab leaves the parked field for the panel). Both verified to fail on the pre-fix modules and pass after.
- Validation: all 19 suites PASS (heat, melt, game, vent, gas, air, simulation, water ×6, calibration, geometry, pressure-propagation, game-balance, vent-balance, render-smoke); `air-scale-fixtures.cjs` still fails on pristine HEAD (pre-existing, untouched). `node --check` and `git diff --check` clean on every touched file.
