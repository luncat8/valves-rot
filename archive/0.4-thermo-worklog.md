## Post-release fixes — vent supply and grain spawn temperature

- Bug (Lab heat): the report read "water and grain should be sourced (spawned at vent) with ambient temperature — currently it looks like 0 and causes water to immediately freeze at the vent even if ambient is high". The supply path already books every parcel at the room temperature, so the spawn was never cold; three rules made it freeze anyway or read cold.
- The freeze rule: a channel froze below its melt plateau (R 120, G 100, B 80 °C), which sits above any ordinary room — so a 20 °C pour froze on arrival at the mouth, and even a 100 °C room still froze the R share (plateau 120). The freeze point is now the LOWER of the plateau and the room: supply spawned at ambient never refreezes on arrival, a hot melt still refreezes as it cools to the room, and a cooler — or a room below the plateau — still freezes a pool; in a room hotter than the plateaus the plateaus themselves govern and the highest-first ordering shows as before. `publish` and the freeze probes take the same point.
- Reset: `H.reset` zeroed the field, so a fresh machine read 0 °C and needed the outer ring's ~36-minute warm-up to reach the room — that is where the "spawned at 0" reading came from. Reset now rebuilds from the water grid's surviving samples (heat's own cached `open` can be older than them) and re-opens the emptied field at the room: `stored == initial` closes on the new opening book, and a pour right after Reset lands on a room-temperature machine instead of a freezing one.
- The capacity base-bleed: `capacityAt`'s `DRY_CAP` nominal was a floor that vanished the moment liquid arrived, so a dry cell's booked base energy was left over a denominator that no longer contained it — every parcel through a transit cell left priced at the inflated blend and bled the base away (the pour band slid below the room and froze). The nominal passage capacity is now kept ALONGSIDE the liquid, in both `capacityAt` and `liveT` (`+ DRY_CAP * open`), so the blend is exact: supply at ambient passing through a cell at ambient leaves it at ambient.
- Creation and first build: `setHeat` now runs `WATER.rebuild` before `HEAT.create` — an unbuilt grid sampled as solid metal everywhere, so the opening book was priced against a phantom capacity and jumped on the first real sample. And water's `rebuild` bumps the serial on the first build too — the first build is the biggest samples change of all, and without the bump a field created before the first build kept its zero-sample capacity until some rotor turned.
- Regression gate (`experiments/vent-supply-temp.cjs`): pours at rooms 20/60/90/100/150 °C on seeds 7 and 42 — nothing freezes on arrival, the mouth reads the room (±0.5 °C), and the books close; poured grains read the room on their cells, and no cell collapses below the design floor min(room, plateaus). That last bound took a probe to settle: the gate's first draft asserted the room itself, which a 150 °C room legitimately violates — the room melts grains and the pinned cells drag their neighbourhood down to the plateaus (a probe measured the distribution: zero cells below 79.5 °C, the pinned cells at exactly 80/100/120, the cooled transit cells between the plateaus and the room, and the books closing at 3.8e-13 relative); the collapsed donors of the bug it was written to catch read ~0 °C, far under either floor. Reset then pour again re-opens at the room and stays liquid, and a cooler at −150 E/s on every valve still froze 5.4 mg into 2 grains below the 20 °C room — freezing exists where it should, it just needs a cell below the room now.
- Fixture updates: heat-fixtures' two reset gates expect the re-opened-at-room stock (`stored == h.initial`, books closed, the field reading the room to 1e-9); melt-fixtures' freeze gates park the room at or above the plateau they drive (`setAmbient` with `shift=false`, a pure setting change), so the plateau — not the room — is the governing freeze point there, and the latent-inventory gate's fill went 300 → 500 °C: the old 300 relied on the dry-crossing pricing bug (the sweep donated the missing excess), while 500 meets the whole-grain threshold by the fill alone.

## Power balance — the scenario/balance script and the default scale

- The standing slice-5 suggestion: a heat scenario/balance script in the water-balance idiom, and a first pass at balancing the default powers and latent heats against the rate ceiling. The previous stage was reviewed first: its floor bound (no cell below its own freeze point) is reused here as the liquid gate, and its "cooler below the room still freezes" rule is what makes the whole cycle measurable. Measured on a deterministic shaft scene (the heat-picture idiom, seed-free): solid-cross rotors at column 3 rows 0-4 open a wide shaft from the pour band into a hollow elbow chamber at (3,5), so a pour at the page's default 40 px²/s lands in the band, falls the shaft and pools in the chamber; the cooler sits on the chamber valve only, the way a player selects it. `experiments/heat-balance.cjs` sweeps the power over 150..20000 with the freeze leg (pour 6 s, cool, first-freeze and frozen@10/20/50 s, the pinned remainder, the liquid floor, the books), the melt-back leg (freeze 30 s, flip the sign, first melt and all-gone, frozen == melted, the returned liquid), the running-pour split at the shipped cooler over rates 40/100/200 (admitted == attempted, nothing freezes in the band — the freeze is a dwell phenomenon, the pour passes the band in under half a second, so the split that matters is shaft vs chamber), the latent knob at the shipped power, and the work ratio derived from the params (the freeze pays the latent plus the capacity swap at the room, the melt pays them at the plateau). Writes `experiments/logs/heat-balance.txt`.
- The measurement: the old default ±150 E/s was inert. A 240 mg pour froze 46 mg in 50 s (19%) and nothing melted back in 200 s — the melt must first accumulate the whole-grain threshold in one cell, and ±150 spread over a valve's 144 cells is ~1 E/s per cell, about an hour for the first grain. The knee: ±3000 melts back in 152 s, ±1500 never finishes. The freeze:melt work ratio is 2.6-3.5x per channel by design, so the melt leg sets the pace and the default has to be tuned against it.
- The tuning: the default scale is now ±5000 E/s (the Power slider's min/max in index.html, step 50 E/s; render-smoke asserts the page and the balance's shipped row move together). At it: first freeze 0.17 s, 111 mg of a 240 mg pour frozen by 20 s, melt-back first melt 49 s / all gone 103 s with frozen == melted and the full 240 px² returned as liquid, and one cooler takes 36% of a running default pour (23% at 100 px²/s, 12% at 200) — the ceiling is a rate, not a share. Latent stays [60, 55, 50]: at the shipped power the freeze plateau is placement-bound, so latent is second-order there (x0.5 -> 101, x1 -> 111, x2 -> 102 mg by 50 s); it was first-order at the old default, where the freeze was still power-bound.
