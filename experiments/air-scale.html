Air scale: three vented cell pipes

Each lane has a bottom vent-in, a vertical cell-valve pipe, a top vent-out, and one grain. Radius and material density together set its mass: the neutral r 2.7 grain is the reference mass that 1× balances, so 1× holds it level while the buoyant blue grain rises and the dense red grain falls; 2× lifts the standard grain too.
