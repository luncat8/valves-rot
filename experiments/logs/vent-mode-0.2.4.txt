rig authority (span = what the seat's two mouths can do to its own pocket)
  every seed: span 1.00..1.00 over columns x rows 3/5/7, sealed leak <= 0.0001/s
  charge 1.00 / drain 0.00 on all 30 column x row x seed combinations tried
  a sealed pocket is a closed volume: it keeps whatever it is left with, which is
  what makes "hold a band" a possible task at all
