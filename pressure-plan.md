# Pressure models

## Goal

Add optional air effects without changing the conservative colored-grain geometry or making them a requirement for gravity flow. Grid gas stores a **normalized relative pressure** `p` in `[0, 1]` and advances it as a conservative diffusion between constant-pressure boundary cells. Its Pa-equivalent ramp is an explicitly scaled visualization, not a thermodynamic pressure measurement.

## Inlets

- Each column has one mouth in the pour band and one in the drain band, drawn as an arrow inside a small disc. The mouth's air cells are **outside the 10×10 play field** (above `y = 0` or below `y = SIZE`).
- Bottom mouths are high (`p = 1`) and top mouths low (`p = 0`). While a mouth is open, all of its band cells are **Dirichlet boundaries**: they hold that value exactly on every solve, forever. The particle backend uses high mouths as sources and low mouths as vents in its recycling circuit. These are not measurements of reservoir pressure.
- Click a mouth to seal or open it; Shift-click swaps its high/low polarity. Reset restores the default assignment. Mouth state survives mode switching and layout changes.
- A sealed mouth is an ordinary air pocket with no pin. With no open mouths at all there is no boundary, so the machine simply **holds the pressure it already has**: no diffusion, no flat repaint, no flow. A single open mouth of either polarity is enough to drive a changing field.

## Air force

- One slider scales how hard either backend pushes grains, 0 to 2 with 1 as the default. At 0 the grains fall exactly as they do with pressure off, which is the regression gate; the fixture asserts an identical drop time.

## Modes

- Radios: Off, Air particles, Grid gas. Default is Grid gas with drawing on.
- Checkbox: Draw air field. Independent of the collision debug overlay and the black/white air background.
- Switching modes replaces only pressure state. Grains, counters and valve targets are preserved. Reset, layout change and valve replacement rebuild the selected backend.

## Air particles

- Dilute population of small elastic discs with independent radius, mass, speed and capacity; no gravity, no grain accounting.
- Swept geometry reflection from housing and moving rotor walls, reciprocal impulses on grains through preallocated bins, bounded by an extreme-speed ceiling. Grain impacts are elastic at air force 1; other slider values change that impulse. No air-air collisions.
- Air is lighter than a grain but not negligible (mass 3 against 7.3 for a 2.7 px grain), so a stream of impacts visibly holds a fall back; the slider scales the exchanged impulse on both bodies.
- Reservoir circuit: air inside a band mouth whose inlet is an open vent is moved to a random open source, aimed into the field. Rate is proportional to the number of open source/vent pairs, so sealing a mouth slows or stops the circulation; the population is constant and cycling is counted.
- Particle drawing is a qualitative air-population view, not a pressure map. Count the normal momentum transferred by actual wall reflections in nominal one-second simulation windows; record the realized duration, normal impulse and hit count. Exclude recovery nudges and the speed-cap impulse. A total impulse is not pressure until a known wall area and time are supplied.

## Grid gas

- 6 px cells over the whole field (`120 × 130` at the current geometry), typed arrays only, solver independent of drawing and DOM. Metal is **not** a cell: only connected air is solved.
- Air mask from `airClearance` plus open faces: an axis-aligned link is sampled against `AIR_MIN = 0.75` — the throat is the smallest clearance along the link, and a wide middle bounds it from below in one sample, so a thin shut rim between two air cells has zero conductance. Links across shut faces and through metal are never taken. Neighbouring air cells are flood-filled into connected components; a component without a mouth pin is a sealed pocket that keeps its own pressure.
- **Aperture faces**: a rotor seam aperture opens off-centre, between two cell centres, so a face is measured across its whole shared edge. The centre line answers most faces in one sample; a blocked face probes six lateral lanes, and a lane counts when the link crosses the rotor rim at an open slot (the exact rim crossings are sampled, which is the one place a thin shut rim cannot slip between samples) and the full throat test confirms the rest of the lane. The face then carries `open lanes / lanes` of its width, scaled by the slot throat against one cell. Without this the graph lost nearly every mouth-connected cell as soon as the rotors left a detent (all 100 valves at 0.12 rad: 1743 → 40 live cells; now 1898). A shut seam still links nothing, and a rotor turned past its ports seals its chamber.
- Face conductance is the harmonic mean of the two cells' openness (air clearance normalized to `AIR_MIN`, capped at 1), reduced by grain occupancy in either cell and by the measured face aperture. Grains therefore raise resistance locally instead of warping a global distance field; a part-open port throttles its link instead of switching it off.
- **Transient**: one backwards-Euler step per 1/30 s mask tick, `cap * (p - p0) = dt * Σ g (p_neighbour - p)` with `dt = 1200`. The constant-pressure mouths are Dirichlet values; every other unknown cell is solved with an IC(0)-preconditioned conjugate gradient on the sparse face graph (up to 400 iterations, relative residual tolerance). The IC(0) factor shares the 5-point stencil and is exact along a 1-D pipe, so a corridor front converges at the physical rate of the step instead of crawling at one cell per iteration; Jacobi needed a sweep per corridor cell and starved long chains. Startup, a snap, and quest probes jump to the steady harmonic solution with the same solver (up to 700 iterations). The linear solve allocates nothing.
- **Front**: pressure starts at a mouth and reaches further cells on later steps — a mouth click crosses a connected machine in about a third of a second, readable as a front rather than an instant repaint. A single mouth click only changes the boundary; the next mask ticks carry the front through open faces. No click or rotor turn repaints the machine. A component with no mouth does not change at all.
- Mask rebuilt when any rotor angle has moved by 0.02 rad (at most once per 1/30 s mask tick), on inlet changes and on valve replacement. A cell's clearance depends only on its own valve and a face's aperture on its two cells' valves, so each is cached with a stamp from those valves: a rebuild re-samples only the cells and faces of rotors that moved (0.08 ms with nothing changed, 0.41 ms with one rotor turning, 2.9 ms with all 100), and an incremental mask is asserted identical to one built from scratch. A rebuild never moves `p`; the following diffusion steps do. The mask clock advances by exactly 1/30 s per tick (keeping phase, dropping only a long stall's backlog), so frame rounding cannot halve the tick rate. A settled machine skips the linear solve entirely, and a paused page keeps the mask clock running so the overlay still answers clicks.
- Gas velocity comes from the **actual face flux** `q = g (p_neighbour - p)`, not a pressure gradient divided by a cell cost, plus the **rigid swirl** of any turning rotor disc: the wall velocity `ω × r` inside the disc is added to the target velocity with no momentum state of its own, so rotation drives local flow, the swirl follows the sign of ω, and it stops a few mask ticks after the rotor does. The swirl also carries one cell (`SWIRL_REACH = 1.5` cells) out through each open port mouth, fading linearly, and nowhere else: a turning rotor stirs the pipe its port opens onto (measured 26 px/s just outside an open mouth, 0 px/s one reach further and 0 for a portless chamber), which is a kinematic wall velocity rather than a momentum state. Flux through a narrow seam is fast; a shut face contributes nothing. The displayed velocity eases toward that target and is capped. Grains in the reservoir bands are left alone.
- Grains feel two empirical couplings, both scaled by air force: drag toward the local gas velocity and a lift term along it. The impulse is subtracted from the cell's velocity (reciprocation, cell mass 1) and clamped to local gas speed. A grain sitting in metal samples the nearest air cell; a disconnected pocket has no flux and therefore no air push. The coupling needs no open mouth when a rotor is turning: rotation, not the pressure reference, is what stirs the air, so only the force slider gates it.
- Scales: on a fully connected cross-valve field a linear 0→1 gradient carries about 90 px/s of mean flow, a 30 px fall takes 0.32 s dry and 0.48 s at air force 1, and air force 2 can hold a grain up. These are tuned gameplay scales, not calibrated gas constants.

## Game pressure quests

- A random machine can leave a goal chamber cut off from both mouth bands. Signing a pressure quest therefore installs a `hollow cross` bottle (open on all four faces, so air can reach it from every passage) and opens a vertical shaft of `solid cross` valves in the bottle's column from the top to the bottom band. The bottle then has a real route to both constant-pressure boundaries.
- `probeMouths` solves the same mouth recipe the bots use (whole favourable band open, one far mouth of the other band as reference) into `pTarget`. `fitPressureQuest` snaps the live field, measures the reachable extremes, and puts the threshold 60% of the way from the current pressure to the chosen extreme, so the quest starts uncommitted and is completable.

## Ramp calibration

- The legend maps `p` linearly to `P_equivalent = p × ΔP_reference`, with low gauge reference 0 and high reference `ΔP_reference`, in Pa. The color ramp remains normalized. A positive editable span can represent another known source-to-vent difference; changing it relabels the ramp without changing the field, gas velocities or grain forces. With no open mouth the ramp still describes the trapped air and the legend names the state (`all mouths sealed`, `no source open`, `no vent open`).
- The default span is an illustrative wall-flux reference, not an in-situ measurement: a sealed circular chamber of wall radius 15 px contains 100 kinetic markers of mass 3 simulation units each, moving at 180 px/s. With 1 mm/px, 1 s per simulation second, 1 mg per mass unit and 10 mm depth, the 20 s reference gives about 0.7125 Pa against a zero-gauge reference. `calibration.js` stores the measurement and converts `impulse / (wall area × elapsed time)` to SI. This choice is not atmospheric pressure or a measurement of either simulated reservoir.
- A unit on the ramp does not make the normalized field, the independently tuned air-force slider, the speed ceiling, or particle reservoirs physically calibrated. Neither absolute nor local thermodynamic pressure is inferred here.

## Overlay and validation

- Relative pressure is drawn as one smoothed texel per gas cell over the passages, tinted by a blue-cyan-amber-red ramp with an equivalent-Pa legend. The air mask keeps metal transparent, and the overlay never disappears while Grid gas is selected.
- `experiments/gas-fixtures.cjs`: 200 constant-pressure band cells outside the field; a connected cross-valve machine with one component and a linear 0–1 gradient; sealing every mouth freezes `p` exactly while keeping its full colour spread and killing the flow; reopening one source and one vent restores it; a 1-D implicit front reaching further cells over later steps and a steady linear profile; a scrambled 301-cell pipe solving in at most 10 sweeps and ten full-strength ticks filling a 121-cell pipe; a mouth change crossing a connected machine within 15 mask ticks with no solve at the iteration cap; a shut chamber that stays empty until its valve is replaced, with no leak through metal; a rotor turn rebuilding the mask without repainting; **a turned machine keeping its air graph** (two angle recipes, live cells within 10% of the aligned state and components within 15%) while a rotor turned past its ports still seals its chamber; a turning rotor driving swirl in its disc and one cell out through an open port, following the sign of ω, stopping with it, and staying inside a portless chamber, on a fully sealed machine; pile occupancy lowering conductance and local flow; grain coupling (force 0 equals off, force 1 slows the fall, force 2 hovers, isolated pockets feel nothing); normalized pressure, bounded velocity, idle solve skipping, mode switching and per-step cost.
- `experiments/calibration-fixtures.cjs`: the production steady kernel against a 1-D analytical serial resistance with a narrow seam at 6 px and 3 px (errors below `1e-4`), a quarter-open face reproducing its own serial resistance, an incremental rebuild identical to a fresh one after rotor turns and a valve replacement, a huge implicit step converging to the same profile without overshoot, and a metal cell splitting a row into two compartments that each hold exactly their own boundary value (plus a pinless compartment keeping its own air). It also samples the real clearance/face predicate at a rim-aligned chord and checks every open runtime face against geometry sweeps across thousands of links — 17,038 faces at four rotation states, 73 of them carried by an off-centre seam, each with at least one lane that crosses no metal at `AIR_MIN`.
- `experiments/aperture-picture.cjs` renders one machine's field from a reference git revision beside the working tree for the visual check (`logs/grid-gas-aperture-0.2.png`): at 0.113 rad on every rotor the previous build kept 6.8% of the air on a mouth in 310 components, this one keeps 59.8% in 78, and the 1.5 px reference keeps 60.5%.
- `experiments/game-fixtures.cjs` and `experiments/render-smoke.cjs` cover fitted pressure quests, the sealed-field behaviour at game level, the always-visible overlay and the legend text. `experiments/simulation-bench.cjs` reports coupling and cost on a mouth-connected chamber.
- Cost is measured per 120 Hz step; pressure off must keep the existing grain behaviour and cost. On Node v22.22.3 the gas fixture measures about 0.19 ms off vs 0.88 ms grid per step with every rotor turning (the previous build measured 0.98 with the same harness), and `simulation-bench.cjs` reports 1.84 ms/60 Hz with 120 grains. A mask rebuild costs 0.08 / 0.41 / 2.9 ms with nothing / one / all rotors turning, at most 30 times a second; `experiments/aperture-probe.cjs` reports the mask against a 1.5 px reference and the rebuild cost.

## Later stages

- To report actual device pressure rather than a normalized relative field, add measured boundary values, a gas equation of state and an energy policy, then validate steady parallel/series flow, transient compression/equalization and local wall-force/area measurements at multiple resolutions.
- Finer grids for sub-cell passages (the mask still needs a cell-wide opening at `AIR_MIN`), and a GPU backend only after the CPU cost is profiled.
- Per-column scaling of the ramp for layouts where one column carries most of the flow.
