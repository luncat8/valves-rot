# Pressure models

## Goal

Add optional air effects without changing the conservative colored-grain geometry or making them a requirement for gravity flow. Grid gas stores a **normalized relative pressure** `p` in `[0, 1]` and advances it as a conservative diffusion between constant-pressure boundary cells. Its Pa-equivalent ramp is an explicitly scaled visualization, not a thermodynamic pressure measurement.

## Inlets

- Each column has one mouth in the pour band and one in the drain band, drawn as an arrow inside a small disc. The mouth's air cells are **outside the 10×10 play field** (above `y = 0` or below `y = SIZE`).
- Bottom mouths are high (`p = 1`) and top mouths low (`p = 0`). While a mouth is open, all of its band cells are **Dirichlet boundaries**: they hold that value exactly on every solve, forever. The particle backend uses high mouths as sources and low mouths as vents in its recycling circuit. These are not measurements of reservoir pressure.
- Click a mouth to seal or open it; Shift-click swaps its high/low polarity. Reset restores the default assignment. Mouth state survives mode switching and layout changes.
- **Game mode** leaves only dealt mouths in the bands: two in (high, bottom) and two out (low, top) per competitor, each in its own random column, so no player's in and out mouths share an `x`. An owned mouth answers only to its owner and keeps the polarity it was dealt; a rival press and an empty slot are refused.
- A sealed mouth is an ordinary air pocket with no pin. With all mouths closed, each connected component conserves its total air, while pressure differences inside it may diffuse; separate components cannot exchange air through shut valves. There is no instantaneous repaint or sustained reservoir through-flow. A single open mouth of either polarity is enough to drive a changing field.

## Air force

- One slider scales how hard either backend pushes grains, 0 to 2 with 1 as the default. At 0 the grains fall exactly as they do with pressure off, which is the regression gate; the fixture asserts an identical drop time.

## Modes

- Radios: Off, Air particles, Grid gas. Default is Grid gas with drawing on.
- Checkbox: Draw air field. Independent of the collision debug overlay and the black/white air background.
- Switching modes replaces only pressure state. Grains, counters and valve targets are preserved. Reset, layout change and valve replacement rebuild the selected backend.

## Air particles

- Dilute population of small elastic discs with independent radius, mass, speed and capacity; no gravity, no grain accounting.
- Swept geometry reflection from housing and moving rotor walls, reciprocal impulses on grains through preallocated bins, bounded by an extreme-speed ceiling. Grain impacts are elastic at air force 1; other slider values change that impulse. No air-air collisions.
- Air is lighter than a grain but not negligible (mass 3 against 7.3 for a 2.7 px grain), so a stream of impacts visibly holds a fall back; the slider scales the exchanged impulse on both bodies.
- Reservoir circuit: air inside a band mouth whose inlet is an open vent is moved to a random open source, aimed into the field. Rate is proportional to the number of open source/vent pairs, so sealing a mouth slows or stops the circulation; the population is constant and cycling is counted.
- Particle drawing is a qualitative air-population view, not a pressure map. Count the normal momentum transferred by actual wall reflections in nominal one-second simulation windows; record the realized duration, normal impulse and hit count. Exclude recovery nudges and the speed-cap impulse. A total impulse is not pressure until a known wall area and time are supplied.

## Grid gas

- The solver state is a **pipe network**, not a world grid. Each valve owns `RS = 8` px rotor-local cells over its disc, sampled once with `passageClearance` in the prims' unrotated frame, plus four static casing corridors (three lanes across, one to three segments along) reaching the tile edges. Adjacent tiles share the far ends of their corridors. A regular 6 px texel grid (`120 × 130`) remains only as the display, grain-coupling and public `index()/p/air` projection; pressure is stored on the network and projected onto those texels.
- **Rotation** updates only the world positions of that valve's rotor cells and its seam links. A rim cell is rotated into the casing frame; if it faces an open analytical seam chord (`v.seam[s] - AIR_MIN`) and a `0.75 px` disc can travel from that cell centre to the static corridor cell, the two are linked. Links are throttled by the remaining chord width and fail closed when the seam shuts. Rotor air is never resampled on a turn; static links inside rotors and casings are cached as each node's first `staticCount` neighbours, so only the turned valves' seam edges are rewritten.
- **No edge crosses metal.** Rotor neighbours are checked with `passageClearance` along their local segment, seam links with `valveClearance` along their short world segment; both are verified independently against machine-wide `G.sweep` in the calibration fixture (500 seam links over four rotation states, 0 blocked). Checking only the cell centres is insufficient around curved elbows.
- Edge conductance is the harmonic mean of the two cells' openness (clearance normalized to `HALF`, clamped to `OPEN_MIN`), scaled by seam aperture and reduced by grain occupancy. A part-open port throttles its links continuously; an unpinned pocket conserves its total pressure while its connected cells equalize.
- **Transient**: sample moving seams and solve once per 120 Hz fixed tick; otherwise use the 30 Hz settling cadence. Inlet and topology changes schedule a solve on the next tick. One backward-Euler step uses `dt = 1200 * min(elapsed, 1/30) / (1/30)` in `cap * (p - p0) = dt * Σ g (p_neighbour - p)`, so four short steps do not each simulate a full 1/30 s. IC(0)-preconditioned conjugate gradient has a 16-iteration budget, compacts unknown edges and samples no geometry inside its iterations. Solve every nonuniform unpinned component as well as pinned ones; bounded per-component correction preserves isolated air despite finite solver tolerance. Uniform components can skip the solve. Steady solves (startup, snap, quest probes) remain pinned-only to avoid a singular system and use a two-BFS distance-ratio warm field: live steady uses 12 polishing iterations, hand-built 1-D fixtures and exact hypothetical mouth sets use up to 700. Explicit sweeps need O(L²) passes to cross a long pipe, so the implicit step is the few-substeps choice.
- **Front**: a mouth click changes only the boundary; the next simulation tick starts a front through open links, without an instant global repaint. A rotor turn carries its rotor-local pressure into new world texels and leaves static casing pressure untouched by the turn itself; diffusion may exchange pressure immediately afterward. Shut faces never leak, and sealed pockets conserve their air while relaxing internal differences.
- Rebuild triggers: any rotor angle changes on a fixed tick, an inlet changes, or a valve is replaced. Static edges and the static part of the display projection are cached; a turn rewrites only that valve's seam edges and rim texels. `calibration-fixtures` asserts an incremental mask identical to one built from scratch over three seeds, seven angle steps and a valve replacement. A settled field skips the linear solve entirely.
- Gas velocity comes from **raw summed edge flux** `q = g (p_neighbour - p)` (degree-normalizing it hid the conductance drop through a pile) plus the **rigid swirl** `ω × r` of a turning rotor, with no momentum state; the swirl fades out through open port mouths. Grains feel drag toward the local gas velocity and lift along it, reciprocated against cell mass 1 and capped at `GAS_MAX`. `FLOW_SCALE = 22000` tunes the empirical coupling: a 30 px fall takes 0.32 s dry, 0.62 s at air force 1 and stalls at force 2.
- Performance is measured per 120 Hz step, including a fully turning machine, rather than assuming the old 30 Hz solve cost still applies. The gas fixture allows `max(3 ms, off * 12)` on Node and `simulation-bench` measures the whole simulation with grains. These are hardware-dependent budgets and tuned gameplay scales, not calibrated gas constants; see `experiments/logs/grid-gas-propagation.txt` for representative measurements.

## Game pressure quests

- A random machine can leave a goal chamber cut off from both mouth bands. Signing a pressure quest therefore installs a `hollow cross` bottle (open on all four faces, so air can reach it from every passage) and opens a vertical shaft of `solid cross` valves in the bottle's column from the top to the bottom band. The bottle then has a real route to both constant-pressure boundaries. A pressure quest always has one chamber: two bottles would sit in two pockets under one shared target, and no single mouth policy has to reach both.
- **Where it goes**: a shaft joins the pocket around its column and the band cells at both ends of it, and what a mouth can do to that pocket depends on who owns its boundary. Sealing an own vent charges a pocket that rivals' vents no longer feed; sealing an own source drains one that rivals' sources no longer fill. `pressurePocketColumns` stamps the pocket a candidate column would join, counts the wanted-polarity mouths in it by owner, and ranks the columns by the signer's share. That is a filter on the air, not on the deal: the bottle's column may carry nobody's mouth, and the pocket keeps whatever rivals share it.
- **How the target is fitted**: `probeMouths(sim, wantHigh, column, owner)` solves only the dealt mouths — the signer's favourable pair open and opposing pair sealed, every other owner's mouth exactly as it stands — into `pTarget`; a column with no dealt mouth has no boundary at all in that probe. `fitPressureQuest` refreshes and snaps the live field, then takes three converged solves: the base field, the charge and the drain. The tier share of the measured `span` (`0.6`, `0.78` on hard) sets the aim, the printed threshold is rounded toward the base so it can never round past the reach, and a target the field already meets is rejected.
- **When the pocket is weak**: a measured span under `0.10` is field drift rather than a lever, so the placement is retried on a fresh row or column (three tries). If nothing clears it, three more columns are played at half aim over whatever weak span they measure (`0.02` minimum), and a board with no lever at all falls back to asking for the side the field already holds. The generated polarity is only flipped in those last two cases.
- **What that costs**: a pocket that mostly answers to rivals moves little when the signer presses, and auto-rotation at `7%` turns enough valves to drift a marginal pocket by a few hundredths. The fit measures both facts instead of inventing headroom, so the policy stays one press — open the pair of the wanted polarity and seal the other pair. `experiments/mouth-quest-probe.cjs` signs and completes `50/50` fitted quests that way (`8 s` window, sign `146 ms` average).

## Ramp calibration

- The legend maps `p` linearly to `P_equivalent = p × ΔP_reference`, with low gauge reference 0 and high reference `ΔP_reference`, in Pa. The color ramp remains normalized. A positive editable span can represent another known source-to-vent difference; changing it relabels the ramp without changing the field, gas velocities or grain forces. With no open mouth the ramp still describes the trapped air and the legend names the state (`all mouths sealed`, `no source open`, `no vent open`).
- The default span is an illustrative wall-flux reference, not an in-situ measurement: a sealed circular chamber of wall radius 15 px contains 100 kinetic markers of mass 3 simulation units each, moving at 180 px/s. With 1 mm/px, 1 s per simulation second, 1 mg per mass unit and 10 mm depth, the 20 s reference gives about 0.7125 Pa against a zero-gauge reference. `calibration.js` stores the measurement and converts `impulse / (wall area × elapsed time)` to SI. This choice is not atmospheric pressure or a measurement of either simulated reservoir.
- A unit on the ramp does not make the normalized field, the independently tuned air-force slider, the speed ceiling, or particle reservoirs physically calibrated. Neither absolute nor local thermodynamic pressure is inferred here.

## Overlay and validation

- Relative pressure is drawn as one smoothed texel per display cell over the passages, tinted by a blue-cyan-amber-red ramp with an equivalent-Pa legend. The air mask keeps metal transparent, and the overlay never disappears while Grid gas is selected.
- `experiments/gas-fixtures.cjs`: fixed-pressure band cells; connected steady gradients; sealed-network conservation and relaxation; 1-D implicit fronts; boundary changes; valve gating and rotor-local pressure transport; swirl; grain occupancy and coupling; normalized pressure, idle solve skipping, mode switching and per-step cost.
- `experiments/pressure-propagation-fixtures.cjs`: next-tick exchange through isolated casing pockets, no leakage across shut rims, snapped-refresh relaxation, fast rotor/casing connections in both directions and at different cadence phases, a seam-width sweep and next-tick exchange for all ported valve types, high and low reservoir openings, elapsed-time scaling, rotating sealed-air conservation, and the display-to-network mapping used for grain occupancy.
- `experiments/calibration-fixtures.cjs`: the production steady kernel against a 1-D analytical serial resistance with a narrow seam at 6 px and 3 px (errors 2.98e-7 / 5.96e-7), a quarter-open face reproducing its own serial resistance, an incremental rebuild identical to a fresh one after rotor turns and a valve replacement, a huge implicit step converging without overshoot, and a metal cell splitting a row into two compartments that each hold their own boundary value. It also validates the live graph: **500 seam links across four rotation states all clear a `G.sweep` at `AIR_MIN`** (340 on off-centre lanes), a solid blank has no seam links, a narrowing seam throttles its conductance monotonically, and a turn never resamples rotor air. Plus the analytical chord and face-stencil checks at 6 px and 3 px.
- `experiments/aperture-probe.cjs` compares the mouth-connected share of the display mask area with a 1.5 px geometric flood fill and reports seam-link validity and rebuild cost. `experiments/front-probe.cjs` measures front coverage and convergence. `experiments/game-fixtures.cjs` and `experiments/render-smoke.cjs` cover pressure-quest gating, sealed-field behaviour, the always-visible overlay and the legend text. `experiments/simulation-bench.cjs` reports coupling and total simulation cost.
- `experiments/logs/grid-gas-propagation.txt` records representative propagation and performance results. `render-smoke` runs the real classic scripts in a VM with a recording canvas/DOM stub; also verify `file://` loading, actual field pixels, fast-turn behavior and runtime errors in a real browser.

## Later stages

- To report actual device pressure rather than a normalized relative field, add measured boundary values, a gas equation of state and an energy policy, then validate steady parallel/series flow, transient compression/equalization and local wall-force/area measurements at multiple resolutions.
- The rotor's local grid is 8 px, so a passage is one cell wide; a finer `RS` would resolve bends better but adds unknowns to every PCG iteration. Coarsening to two cells per tile would make explicit sweeps affordable again. A GPU backend only after the CPU cost is profiled.
- Per-column scaling of the ramp for layouts where one column carries most of the flow.
