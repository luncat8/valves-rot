Combined / Rotary Valve Field

One geometry layer drives drawing and collision, and one fixed-step conservative solver drives the grains. Double-click to replace a valve (clears its grains); Ctrl-click to fill with one random color. Click a valve or focus the field and use arrow keys plus Enter/Space to turn a rotor. The mouths in the pour and drain bands are air inlets: bottom rows are high, top rows low. Click a mouth to seal or open it, Shift-click to swap its polarity. Air force scales the push on grains; at 0 they fall as if pressure were off.

Grid colors: relative potential (calibration details)

The grid remains a relative potential, not a measured pressure. The legend maps 0–1 to 0–the reference span in gauge-equivalent pascals; changing the span only relabels the ramp and does not change forces. With no open air mouth the field is unanchored and hidden.

The default span is measured from normal particle-wall impulse in a sealed reference chamber: 100 markers at 180 px/s, radius 15 px, over 20 simulation seconds. Its illustrative scales are 1 mm/px, 1 simulation second = 1 s, 1 mg per mass unit (3 mg per marker), and 10 mm depth. It is not an atmospheric pressure or a validation of pressure everywhere in the machine. Enter a known source-to-vent gauge difference to use another reference.
