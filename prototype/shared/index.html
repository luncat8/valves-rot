Help

Playing

One geometry layer drives drawing and collision, and one fixed-step conservative solver drives the grains. Click or press-drag a valve — or focus the field and use arrow keys plus Enter/Space — to turn a rotor. A press-drag dials one 90° detent per quarter turn of the hand: its arc shows the pull, its bold notch the detent the rotor is committed to, and a short wobble never turns it. Pressing near the hub pulls horizontally instead, one detent per 52 px. Auto-rotate sets the share of valves that turn automatically on their schedule (90% default in Lab mode, 7% in Game mode).

In Game mode (G), sign an open quest to spawn one or two upright hollow single · direction ↑ N · 270° (ports N) goal bottles tinted in your player color (a pressure quest uses a four-way bottle). Build a route with the valves, press Pour for grains or connect a pressure source or sink to the target, and hold its count or local pressure to score. Pressure targets stay as advertised: no column is opened for you. Air from any open mouth can be routed, including a rival's, but only its owner can operate that mouth. Isolate competing branches when needed. Finished bottles become solid blank plugs and a new quest replaces the completed one.

The mouths in the pour and drain bands are constant-pressure boundary cells outside the 10×10 field: bottom rows hold high (1), top rows low (0). In Lab mode every column has a mouth: click one to seal or open it, Shift-click to swap its polarity. In Game mode the bands are dealt to the competitors instead — two in (source) mouths at the bottom and two out (vent) mouths at the top per player, at independent random columns and tinted in the owner's color — and only the owner can press them. A player's four mouths never share a column, and an in mouth never sits above that player's own out mouth; one column can still carry one player's source and another player's vent. Air only travels through open valve faces, so a shut port traps the pressure behind it.

Controls and air

Turn speed controls valves, not gravity. Step advances one step while paused. Switching the pressure mode does not reset grains or valve targets.

Grid gas solves a conservative diffusion of relative pressure through the valve pipes. Each rotor's passages are divided into cells in the rotor's own turning coordinates, while the short casing pipes stay still; a turn refreshes the seam links on each simulation tick. Open mouths are fixed at 1 or 0; air spreads through links that a small clearance disc can actually cross, so metal never leaks and a part-open port throttles the flow instead of snapping shut. Turning a valve carries the air inside its rotor with it, and even isolated connected pipes exchange pressure. With every mouth sealed, each disconnected pocket keeps its air while differences inside it gradually even out; the field is not instantly repainted. A packed pile raises resistance, and a turning rotor swirls air in its own disc and just outside open ports. Air particles shows a cyan kinetic population that circulates from bottom sources to top vents and knocks grains about. Off leaves the grains untouched. Air force scales the push on grains from 0 (they fall as if pressure were off) to 2 (updrafts can hold a grain).

Lubricant keeps a packed passage moving. Positional contacts alone cannot carry the weight of a bed, so without it a full shaft arches over a bend and stops: the grains stack, and neither gravity nor air gets them through the corner. At 1 (default) grains that are already touching a neighbour get a rare deterministic sideways knock — the granular temperature a shaker would add — so a saturated pipe creeps, an elbow lets its charge through, and a horizontal run can be pushed by air or a lower outlet. At 0 a packed bed locks the way friction alone would; at 2 the shake is obvious.

Grid colors: relative pressure

The grid stores a normalized relative pressure, not a measured thermodynamic one. The legend maps 0–1 to 0–the reference span in gauge-equivalent pascals; changing the span only relabels the ramp and does not change forces. With no open mouth the ramp still describes the trapped air; the legend names the missing source or vent when only one polarity is open.

The default span is measured from normal particle-wall impulse in a sealed reference chamber: 100 markers at 180 px/s, radius 15 px, over 20 simulation seconds. Its illustrative scales are 1 mm/px, 1 simulation second = 1 s, 1 mg per mass unit (3 mg per marker), and 10 mm depth. It is not an atmospheric pressure or a validation of pressure everywhere in the machine. Enter a known source-to-vent gauge difference to use another reference.

Other info

The line under the field reports fps, the seed, active / injected / drained grains, plus solver diagnostics: purged (removed by Drain or by replacing a valve), recovered and pushes (grains nudged out of a turning wall). In grid mode it also shows mean pressure and flow.

Valve type names (solid / hollow × straight, elbow, tee, cross, single, blank) are listed in the Place valve drawer, where each name can be copied to the clipboard as text.
